## Preserving the scientific measurement contract

A prospective learning-rate feasibility note erroneously said an invalid action ends the whole comparison. Independent inspection of the production evaluator and auditor at scientific source revision `4a9556375e4c299c26dc5abd07f7325b9587a132` confirms a narrower rule: if the parser does not select an action, that episode receives `selection_failure` and outcome 0, after which evaluation can continue. Selected-action/map inconsistency, truncated results or technical and evidence failures are distinct conditions that stop the comparison with unresolved outcomes preserved. The engineering one-step gate is not a replacement for this scientific episode rule. A future change would be a protocol amendment, not a faithful rerun.

## Decision and numerical sensitivity

Current priority is to establish a lawful, demonstrably bounded development source, a decision whose possible outcomes change research investment, appropriate measures of statistical precision and a full prospective resource ledger. The task-source and independent-confirmation questions remain open. Do not use protected tasks or their derived associations to search for or manufacture a safe input. A same-recipe training repeat is potentially more directly diagnostic of the current one-product limitation than an unmotivated learning-rate search, but one repeat cannot establish seed stability. This is an option to investigate, not a released experiment.

For illustration only, assume one fixed model pair and 82 independent hypothetical paired outcomes. For a rule requiring at least five net wins, exact multinomial enumeration gives advancement probability 5.68% at no true difference and 43.43% at +5 percentage points when discordance probability is 10%; with 20% discordance, the respective values are 13.22% and 45.85%. These are sensitivity calculations for an unapproved threshold, not actual outcome probabilities, a power guarantee or evidence that 82 independent eligible parents exist. Thresholds and success criteria require an independent prospective rationale; repeated tuning changes their interpretation.
