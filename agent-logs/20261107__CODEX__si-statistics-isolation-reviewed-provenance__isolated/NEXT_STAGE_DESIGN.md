# C-low next stage: task independence, precision and complete budget

Status: **prospective design only; no evaluation, protected task access or new experiment.** The current C-low product has independently checked inner completion and a final adapter, but its outer supervision is UNKNOWN with exit 1; the result is `INNER_COMPLETED_OUTER_UNKNOWN_NO_RETRY`. Preserve that status and all costs. Any development use of this artifact needs an explicit, reviewed lineage/use decision that binds both inner and outer evidence; it must never be relabeled an overall successful supervised run. The previously exposed development tasks, including the known pool of 82, are not an untouched confirmation set. There is no known extra independent asset. The design below does not assert that suitable unseen tasks already exist.

## Two stages, two very different questions

1. **Feasibility only:** the predesignated two exposed development tasks and three fixed arms (P0, historical C-high, and C-low, conditional on the above use decision) produce six supervised episodes. This checks product lineage, exact same P0 prompt/parser, task loading, end-to-end accounting, output persistence and outcome auditing. It can expose a counterexample, but two parent tasks cannot establish a success-rate improvement, reliable precision or generalization. Existing witnesses must be retained separately and unavailable to the actor; do not retry old completed or incomplete units as fresh work. Fix protocol/actual prompt validation mismatch and six-unit owner/product provenance before any run. Report outcomes and technical UNKNOWN separately, not as a formal result.
2. **Conditional confirmatory evaluation:** first demonstrate that enough genuinely unexposed parent tasks can be created and held blind. Freeze one primary endpoint and pairing before any evaluation: per independent parent, whether C-low succeeds by horizon 50 versus whether unchanged P0 succeeds on the same parent under the same frozen actor/environment conditions. This comparison addresses the self-improvement success-rate objective. C-high versus P0 and C-low versus C-high are prespecified secondary contrasts; do not present nominal secondary p-values as confirmatory without a prospective multiple-testing rule. A qualified C-low with outer UNKNOWN requires that provenance caveat in every report. If these prerequisites cannot be met, keep all future work labeled exploration and do not claim confirmatory evidence.

## Actual task isolation

No protected task identities, contents or witnesses are to be opened to the researcher or used to choose tasks. A separate custodian should create new same-domain tasks from a versioned, frozen generator and genuinely distinct parent causal structures, not merely variations of filenames, object IDs, seeds, room initializations or surface text. Record parent lineage, causal family and generation parameters in a private registry; freeze the generator, target population, positive and negative constraints, family proportions and seed allocation **before** exposing evaluation outcomes. Static validity and environment operability may be checked by an independent custodian, with any scripted witnesses stored outside actor-readable inputs and not used to guide the evaluated model. A hidden deterministic case bundle and exact byte commitment should be frozen before models are run; distribute only the minimum environment files needed for each locked evaluation unit. Hash commitments do not hide low-entropy content; use high-entropy secret salt for blinded commitments and keep the salt with the custodian until reveal.

Maintain an append-only development-use/holdout registry and audited access logs. An independent custodian compares new parents against all historical known-use records and evaluation exclusions, including the 82 exposed development parents, by exact identity and by preregistered semantic/causal normalization; require both deterministic collision checks and documented manual review for ambiguous structural overlaps, without revealing the protected comparison contents or identities. A blinded overlap answer is only as complete as the accessible history: record coverage, algorithm, reviewer and unresolved cases. Missing historical inventory, inability to check semantic overlap, witness exposure, later outcome-driven generator changes or shared information about individual holdout cases means **independence UNKNOWN** and no confirmatory designation. Parent assignment and arms must not be adjusted using results; freeze a batch/analysis plan and guard against the same underlying parent appearing in both development and confirmation. Separate custodian roles, least-privilege storage, and sealed reveals must be implemented, not just asserted in JSON.

If cases are generated from a few templates, the independent statistical units are their genuinely independent parent structures, not the number of renamed instances or model steps; strong relatedness across parents can reduce the effective sample size. Even with a valid fresh set, the inference targets the prespecified **generated same-domain task distribution**, not all ALFWorld tasks or unseen environments. Source/author overlap and distribution shift are limitations. If only one generator and no trusted blind custodian are available, the result remains exploratory.

## Statistics: explicit planning examples, not observed effects

For each fresh parent define `n10` = C-low succeeds and P0 fails, `n01` = P0 succeeds and C-low fails. The paired success-rate difference is `(n10-n01)/N`. For a single prespecified primary two-sided test at alpha .05, use the exact conditional McNemar/binomial test: conditional on `M=n10+n01`, the null is Binomial(M, 0.5). Report the effect, a prespecified paired 95% confidence interval, all four paired cells and denominator; an exact-binomial p-value alone is not an interval or a measure of practical value. Do not choose a one-sided test after outcomes.

Assuming independent parents and, solely for sizing, probabilities `Pr(n10)=0.20`, `Pr(n01)=0.05` (true paired difference +15 percentage points; discordance 25%), exact unconditional power for the conditional two-sided test is approximately 73.7% at N=80 and 90.4% at N=120. Under this hypothetical model, the first N reaching 80% power is 92. For the smaller assumed difference `0.15 - 0.05 = +10` points with 20% discordance, power is about 43.7% at N=80, 63.3% at N=120, 77.7% at N=160 and 87.5% at N=200; the first N reaching 80% is 168. These are illustrations, **not measured effect sizes**. Different concordance, dependence, task selection, technical missingness or multiplicity changes power. An exact conditional test is discrete, so power can have local irregularities; recalculate after freezing assumptions and rule.

To show precision rather than promise power, suppose—not an actual result—that 80 parents yield `n10=16`, `n01=4`; the point estimate is +15 points and the familiar approximate paired Wald 95% interval is +4.5 to +25.5 points, with exact two-sided conditional p≈0.0118. If 120 parents produce 24 and 6 respectively, the same illustrative effect has approximate Wald interval +6.5 to +23.5 points and p≈0.00143. These intervals are **normal approximations for illustration**, not exact or guaranteed-coverage intervals, and do not establish practical importance. Before data collection specify a suitable paired-score or inverted exact procedure with defensible coverage, a meaningful target effect and interval width; include zero-discordant handling, parent clustering/dependence, missingness and family stratification. A reasonable candidate design to discuss is N≈200 independent parents if a +10-point alternative and the stated discordance are defensible, but it is not a commitment or an observed power estimate.

## Full budget before admission

For N paired parent tasks, three arms and one attempt each, the structural ceiling is 3N episodes, 150N generations and 153N reset-plus-step operations; generation caps imply 614,400N input and 2,400N output tokens. For 2 parents these are 6 episodes, 300 generations, 306 reset/steps, 1,228,800 input and 4,800 output tokens. For N=80: 240 episodes, 12,000 generations, 12,240 reset/steps, 49,152,000 input and 192,000 output tokens. For N=120: 360 episodes, 18,000 generations, 18,360 reset/steps, 73,728,000 input and 288,000 output tokens. Each also needs up to 3N environment constructions/loads/closures and model-load/adapter-load counts dictated by a frozen execution schedule, plus tokenization, data validation, GPU allocation, source/tree hashes, process control, artifact storage, failure cleanup and audits. All prior training, prior failed operations, preparatory witnesses and unknown tails are added once with scope reconciled; do not substitute upper bounds for actual costs or mark unresolved work zero.

A transparent I/O planning formula is `number_of_base_hashes × S + number_of_model_loads × L + adapter_reads + package/source_reads + environment_inputs + output_and_audit_IO`, where `S=16,381,516,776` bytes is the known five-shard logical byte sum and L is the actually measured or explicitly reserved logical load allowance. Under the **conditional** one-S-per-unit hash and one-S-per-unit load allowance, 3N episodes require 6N×S: approximately 183.08 GiB for two parents, 7,323.11 GiB for 80 and 10,984.66 GiB for 120, excluding extras. These are logical sizing assumptions, not measured physical reads or a hard I/O cap; the old 48 GiB per arm cannot fund even two parents under these assumptions. A separately validated persistent per-arm model/reuse design could reduce repeated loads, but it changes process boundaries, contamination/cleanup and proof requirements and must be reviewed before being budgeted as fact. Likewise calculate wall and device budget as the sum of actual bounded per-arm setup plus per-episode time, audit, failures and cleanup; do not invent a duration from token counts. Record free storage, measured output growth, telemetry sampling and overshoot; no unimplemented quota, energy meter or physical-I/O hard cap may be claimed.

## Pre-registration, validity and stops

Before any real unit, bind the task-set commitment, complete protocol and actor/runtime hashes, arm products and provenance, fixed arm/case ordering with a balanced predeclared allocation to limit order effects, seeds, all prior cost, generation/reset/token/time/device/storage/IO plans, independent review and output/cleanup custody. Verify that every arm sees the same task/initial state and common renderer under the frozen endpoint. Define the minimal scientifically meaningful improvement and the CI precision criterion before data; stop for futility only with a predeclared design and valid error control, not by ad hoc early peeking.

Technical failure, unexpected actor input, model/ALFWorld error, unclear external return/cleanup, lost provenance, incomplete cost, leaked holdout/witness, broken blind, exceeded budget or invalid device/source is STOP. Genuine malformed selected actions are policy failures only if the frozen endpoint declares them and the production parser/ledger proves the classification. Do not automatically retry, backfill or drop hard cases. Report UNKNOWNs and all completed costs; if some paired outcomes are missing, provide predeclared worst/best-case bounds and sensitivity rather than treating UNKNOWN as failure or deleting it. A paused or incomplete campaign is not a null or confirmatory negative.

Minimum real path: (a) settle C-low's outer-UNKNOWN provenance and prospective filter contract; (b) decide whether B/C-only six-unit engineering feasibility is worth its full budget and build/test a real single-use owner; (c) independently identify a custodian and prove availability/independence of a sufficiently large blinded parent pool; (d) freeze and review statistical protocol and full campaign budget; only then authorize an appropriate evaluation. If (c) cannot be demonstrated, report an exploratory thesis with transparent limitations and continue sourcing tasks rather than claim a confirmatory effect.
