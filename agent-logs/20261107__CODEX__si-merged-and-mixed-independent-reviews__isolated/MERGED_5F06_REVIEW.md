The test suite imported CPU-side libraries but made no model, GPU, real environment, protected-task, network, or scientific execution call. Tests execute in isolated subprocesses where process-level subreaper state matters.

- On the actual tracked path, a valid synthetic episode reaches the accepted receipt and naturally exits. When a separately constructed worker sends its final receipt and closes before receiving the ACK, the owner sees the broken ACK, does not treat the run as complete, stops the registered child, and the worker's deliberately delayed fake effect never occurs. The 50-step contradictory-terminal counterexample is rejected and its corrected counterpart accepted. Do not infer a trustworthy separate custodian or fresh held-out task supply from this implementation.
