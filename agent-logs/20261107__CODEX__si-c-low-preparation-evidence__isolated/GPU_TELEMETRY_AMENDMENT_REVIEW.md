# Independent scientific review: prospective shared-GPU telemetry amendment

Reviewed private amendment SHA256 `2f1bb2d1c375ef446b73d3cc939267d5fa1bb4529626db3211b93f2ffc05fee8`. It is explicitly prospective for one exploratory C-low attempt, after the static preflight and before live work. This is a scientific/operational assessment only; no model, training, GPU sampler or environment was invoked and no execution clearance is conferred.

The text precisely changes the previous proposal's device identification and periodic telemetry obligations: it uses the full consistent GPU UUID rather than PCI bus ID, retains prelaunch UUID/free/utilization admission, and retains runtime UUID/free, loss/gap and low-free stop policies. It openly designates unmeasured live used memory, temperature, power, utilization and energy UNKNOWN, disclaims exclusive access, attribution and GPU-efficiency claims, and retains the original training/allocator, one-attempt, time, input and storage policies. For an exploratory training product on a shared device, accepting that narrower evidence is scientifically coherent provided no inference is made about power, utilization, execution efficiency or device exclusivity. Its statement that missed samples and stop latency remain unknown is appropriate.

Risks remain: other workloads may arrive immediately after admission; later changes in device load, thermal behavior and power cannot be characterized from the reduced telemetry, may affect throughput, the wall deadline, completion or interference, and would limit comparisons. A shared-device operator should stop on observed telemetry or resource-guard failure and report incomplete work; it must not kill unrelated processes. The reviewed UUID identifies the selected reported device but says nothing about host namespace ownership or PCI topology. Exact actual resource behavior and device-side energy remain unknown; no outcome is implied.

The amendment supplies an honest prospective policy rather than retroactive evidence and does not change the declared training treatment. I find no separate scientific blocker to accepting this explicitly scoped reduction for the single exploratory attempt, contingent on independent acceptance of the actual device admission, controller and process cleanup. Before actual dispatch, an external reviewer must bind this exact amendment digest alongside the actual enabled adapter and its reviewed binding, fresh clearance, preflight, admission, monitor conditions and one-run identity. The historical production clearance schema does not itself contain this amendment; a self-computed review digest is not an independently signed approval. This review does not provide that live clearance.
