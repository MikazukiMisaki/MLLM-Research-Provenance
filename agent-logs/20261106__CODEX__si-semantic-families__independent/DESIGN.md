# Same-domain semantic family candidates for a narrower transfer study

Static design from installed public ALFWorld domain/grammar sources only. No historical task was inspected and no PddlEnv, model or scientific evaluation was run. Neither a working dataset nor an independent benchmark is established.

The domain supports distinguishing an instance goal `inReceptacle(o,destination)` from a conjunction `isClean(o) AND inReceptacle(o,destination)`, using its existing `CleanObject` operator, not an additional environment. A candidate compositional question could construct separate, independently authored generator records for one goal object versus two goal objects, within both place and clean-then-place families. Assign the one-object semantic components to train and the two-object components to evaluation. The `holdsAny`/single-hand conditions support the qualitative expectation that two different objects require separate transfer actions, while cleaning adds a sink action; actual parsing, reachability, action behavior and instructions have not been verified.

The target estimand would be narrow one-to-two-object out-of-combination transfer, not an IID test or random held-out benchmark. In this deliberately conservative candidate grouping, `(goal family, goal-object cardinality)` is a **merging key**, not merely a stratification label: all examples with one key form a single coarse component. There are thus four coarse components in all and only two in the proposed evaluation, one per family. Repeated instances cannot supply independent component-level replication; this cannot provide a reliable across-component precision estimate. Freeze the estimand as a finite-set conditional contrast and report component-level counts and descriptive results, not precision derived from treating variants as independent. Rename-only variants, seeds, open-state flips and distractor counts must not be treated as independent components. Common parameterized generator rules do not automatically establish contamination, but reused concrete tasks or trajectories, common concrete parent instances or documented cross-key lineage must merge or exclude the linked examples; if a component then straddles splits, reject the split. These grouping and lineage policies require justification before outcomes and do not establish ground truth independence.

For a C1/C4 comparison, predeclare a target-label property separately from family/split. Define M and K as counts of distinct eligible training **loss examples** after frozen deduplication, rather than task or component counts; verify `0<K<M`, and separately report support by family/component and label. A structural label invented only to force balance tests the weighting interface, not failure-directed self-improvement. A substantive failure-directed claim could use a preregistered training-side criterion derived from permitted frozen P0 behavior, including model outputs and environment outcomes, provided collection, classification, quality filtering, budgets and lineage are fixed in advance and all attempts are retained. Never use evaluation feedback, C1/C4 outcomes, post-comparison relabeling or post-hoc selection. Any evaluation label is not training input. Assign all descendants together under the chosen lineage rule. C1/C4 must share actual permitted training samples, P0 start, ordering and dose, with weighting as the declared difference. Report per family/component/label examples, active tokens, weight totals and optimization exposure; multi-step trajectories and repeated examples change those quantities. Variants in one component remain correlated and do not increase independent components. Synthetic expert traces must not be presented as self-collected experience.

Validity requires two distinct pickupable targets for the two-object case, a conjunction of the two distinct target predicates, valid locations/receptacles and `canContain` facts, a real initially-unsatisfied goal for each, and consistent single-hand initial facts. The domain does not enforce a valid initial state: explicitly check each object has one valid containment or holder, and `holdsAny` agrees with the unique agent's `holds` facts. The clean variant additionally needs the appropriate `cleanable`, sink type, holding and location facts, with each target initially not clean. Public grammar includes take, go, clean and move templates, but the existing generic task intro is `UNKNOWN GOAL`: a correct instance-specific instruction and consistent name/action mapping would need explicit verification through the production adapter. Do not silently substitute or claim equivalence to benchmark instructions. These families and their variants may be invalid or unreachable; all failures, incomplete work, grouping conflicts and unknowns must be preserved.

Separately generated self-authored records can support a limited within-project construction and documented split. They do not establish non-overlap with unknown historical data, absence of model pretraining exposure, benchmark generalization or causal mechanism. Changing cardinality also changes steps, language, horizon difficulty and goal complexity; a C4/C1 outcome concerns the whole training recipe on that shift, not a uniquely identified reasoning mechanism. A single product per arm cannot estimate training-seed or product variability. Full data yield, fixed analysis, physical budget, training and evaluation precision remain unresolved.
