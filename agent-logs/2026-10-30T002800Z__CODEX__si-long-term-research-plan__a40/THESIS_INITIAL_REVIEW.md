Add [3] after the benchmark and [4] after the model, ideally include experiment-specific configuration in a methodology citation to project evidence. Say that the reconstruction found all 2,206 recorded outputs selected a valid action and that each reconstructed selection matched the recorded executed request, in this selected subset; note that this does not prove other pipeline errors absent. The preceding measured statement already gives the useful evidence; neither reconstructed parsing nor matched request establishes correctness of behavior.

Connect numerical results and cost claims to locally verified experiment/protocol references and a shareable appendix, and finish broader scholarly coverage and supervisor review.
