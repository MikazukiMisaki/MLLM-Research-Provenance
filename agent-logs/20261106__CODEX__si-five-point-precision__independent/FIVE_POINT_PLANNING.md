# Two-contrast 5-percentage-point precision: planning only

For two prespecified paired comparisons, D in [-1, 1], independent task draws from the frozen target distribution, simultaneous two-sided 95% coverage by Bonferroni plus Hoeffding has half-width sqrt(2 ln(80)/n). A sufficient integer n for a half-width of at most 0.05 is ceil(2 ln(80)/0.05²) = **3,506**; the bound at that n is 0.0499973. This applies conditionally to fixed checkpoints and the frozen constructed-task distribution under the stated sampling and complete-outcome assumptions. It does not demonstrate independence of the available tasks, empirical variance, statistical power, affordability or across-family generalization.

For P0, C1 and C4 evaluated once each on the same n tasks, assuming horizon 50, no more than one generation and one step per turn, and one reset per episode, the full evaluation ceiling is **525,900 model generations** and **536,418 reset-plus-step operations**. Adding the prior purely illustrative acquisition envelope of 2,000 generations and 2,530 reset-plus-step operations gives **527,900 generations** and **538,948 reset-plus-step operations**. Construction, task verification and rejections, model loads, training, token limits, CPU/GPU/device time, cleanup, monitoring, incomplete operations and unknown liabilities remain additional and unbounded by these counts. This arithmetic does not authorize or imply feasibility of such a run.

R19's reported 5.56 percentage points is an ex-post oracle upper-envelope difference on a different sample and intervention. It is not an established self-improvement treatment effect and cannot be used as a predicted effect size for this three-arm study. A nominal ±5-point interval is not an 80% power guarantee for a 5.56-point effect, nor does it ensure exclusion of zero or a prespecified minimum-benefit boundary. A power calculation requires a separately specified effect, paired discordance and decision rule, including multiplicity and the desired rejection region.
