# Independent review — attenuated progress-weighted self-imitation

**Assessment: a reasonable exploratory ablation with a clear treatment, subject to tokenization, exact implementation and resource checks.** Compared with the previous stronger linear vector, `w_j = 0.5 + (j+1)/(n+1)` reduces extreme suppression of early decisions. Both arms would use the **same per-example mean completion-token loss**, then average 12 examples per update; one arm has all weights 1 and the other the fixed positional weights. The only specified training difference between those two arms is the weight vector. This is a new adaptive research proposal after inspecting prior source outcomes, not an independent confirmatory result or evidence that eventual-success suffixes are optimal.

For each parent of n decisions, `sum_j w_j=n` and `0.5<w_j<1.5`; every example remains positively weighted. On the existing aggregate of 99 examples across 10 parents, exact normalized row-distribution total variation from uniform is `6535/57834 ≈ 0.113`. For the longest parent, n=26, the first and last weights are `29/54 ≈ 0.537` and `79/54 ≈ 1.463`, ratio `79/29 ≈ 2.724`; this is non-vacuous and milder than the prior 26:1 rule. These are deterministic weight facts, not a predicted performance improvement. Row position must be the original consecutive decision index within the complete successful episode. Missing/ineligible intermediate steps do not authorize silently reindexing a different subset.

## Interpretation and remaining confounds

- Each parent's full-pass **nominal example coefficient** sums to its original sample count, even if completion-token lengths differ, because each sample loss is averaged before weighting. Same 99 examples, four exact passes and fixed order ensure the full-pass coefficient sum is the same in both arms. That does not hold automatically for each 12-row optimizer update: parent mix and total weights per update can change. Different token contents, gradient directions, clipping and nonlinear AdamW state also change effective influence. Save per-example active-token counts, weights, per-update weight sums, parent coefficients, gradient norms and clipping events; do not claim identical gradient dose or a pure behavioral mechanism.
- This **example-mean** objective is different from the historical C-quarter continuation trainer's active-token-weighted mean. Applying it identically to uniform and progress arms gives an interpretable comparison between those two arms; either arm versus uncontinued C-quarter reflects the whole new replay procedure, not positional weighting alone. Check the frozen runtime's actual causal loss reduction and compare the new uniform implementation against an explicit per-example loss reference. Do not silently call it equivalent to the historical token-mean objective when label lengths differ.
- Later events are selected from trajectories that eventually succeeded; later is not necessarily more causal, useful or correct. The two inspected failures do not acquire a validated corrective target from this reweighting. The intervention could also reduce learning of early search behavior. A positive or negative small DEV result cannot isolate why it arose.
- Reconstruct and freeze the same 99 prompts, bare-ID labels+EOS, token IDs, attention/label masks, per-parent step order, starting C-quarter adapter and base, optimizer/seed and four-pass schedule for both arms. The original logged response was decoded/stripped and original token IDs/rendered-prompt hash were not retained: this is a new encoding, not recovery of original inference tokens. Require exact token prefix, completion-only causal shift, valid EOS, no unreviewed truncation and <=2,048 combined length for all 99; stop on any missing/mismatched row rather than replace it. Neither the source nor this review has established actual tokenizer feasibility.

## Smallest useful prospective decision

Freeze in advance a disjoint, source-traceable later 12-parent DEV panel and environment seeds without inspecting those parents' outcomes. Parent/task identity separation from all 99 training sources is necessary but not evidence that an already exposed DEV pool is independent confirmation. Compare uncontinued C-quarter, uniform replay, and progress-weighted replay on the same 12 parents with predetermined balanced arm order and H50. Require 36/36 completed and valid; preserve UNKNOWN and stop without retry/backfill.

The most informative minimal readout is the **paired progress-versus-uniform gain/loss table**, together with both-success/both-failure and each arm's counts. Also report uniform-versus-original and progress-versus-original so any effect of replay itself is visible. A predeclared exploratory resource screen may require progress minus original >=2/12 **and** progress minus uniform >=1/12, with all records complete. These are resource-selection thresholds, not significance tests or causal proof; one case is 8.33 percentage points, and one net discordant gain cannot establish a statistical advantage. Failure means this exact development screen did not clear; it is not proof that self-improvement is generally ineffective. A pass warrants separate preregistered confirmation on defensible independent parents and multiple training seeds, not a thesis-level generalization claim on its own.
