## Search method and scope

Four search clusters: (`ALFWorld`, `agent`, `expert demonstrations`, `self-generated`, `success`, `supervised fine-tuning`); (`language agent`, `expert vs on-policy`, `rejection sampling`, `controlled comparison`); (`self-training`, `successful trajectories`, `reflection`); and (`deep reinforcement learning`, `random seeds`, `statistical uncertainty`, `few runs`). Forward/backward reading used author papers in ACL Anthology, NeurIPS proceedings, PMLR and arXiv full text; searched October 2026, prioritizing same environment and direct empirical comparisons. A focused selection of 11 primary papers is represented below; original 2024 Re-ReST and 2025 Sarukkai papers are retained for context. The search is neither exhaustive nor a defined systematic review; source rankings and search engine coverage are not comprehensive. For publication status, entries explicitly marked arXiv are preprints in the inspected source; publication venue not assumed. Claims are paper descriptions and author-reported comparisons, not independently reproduced findings or directly comparable to our measurements.

SI-U1 baseline for contrast: same expert-supervised Qwen3-8B P0; A unchanged; B expert-demonstration continuation; C P0-success-trajectory continuation; B/C each 78 parent groups, 312 samples and 6,240 supervised tokens, but different source tasks, states and input token totals. Fixed paired 82 TRAIN tasks, one trained seed per continuation, incomplete historical scene isolation; observed result inconclusive. Exact evaluation examples and synthetic task data are not included in this literature review.

## Implications for the thesis

1. Cite Few-Shot Butlers as direct prior work. The broad idea of using an expert-initialized text-game agent's successful trajectories for further action-model training predates this thesis. The project must not claim that this setup, success-only filtering, or experience-based self-training in ALFWorld is its first invention.
2. The honest immediate contribution is a **bounded empirical replication/contrast and failure audit** under a clearly stated common P0, paired task schedule and matched supervision-dose configuration. These are design features, not established unique methodological novelty; the sources do not demonstrate that this exact combination is unprecedented.
3. B and C originate from different tasks/states as well as different sources; the observed policy contrast cannot identify source as the only cause. Failed episodes, sparse double-object coverage and repetition are hypotheses/observations, not demonstrated mechanisms. A success bit is a selection condition, not a guarantee every training action is useful.
4. A master's-thesis contribution may still rest on careful comparison, negative/inconclusive results and reproducible analysis, subject to supervisor and institutional judgment. The current result does not show successful self-improvement or estimate its stability across model-training seeds. No literature paper can replace the missing independence, precision or future empirical evidence.
5. Before thesis submission, broaden retrieval systematically, verify final venue/version metadata (especially preprints and the COLM title difference), cite the above work in the relevant sections, and preserve documented differences in sampling, feedback, action interfaces, checkpoint selection, supervision and budgets. This review does not prescribe new runs or override existing research gates.
