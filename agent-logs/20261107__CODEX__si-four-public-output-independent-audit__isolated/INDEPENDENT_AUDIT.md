## Publication and model-input handling

The generated problems, grammars, full specs, structural signatures and especially `*.witness.json` reveal task content/solutions. They are self-authored development artifacts, not protected historical tasks, but publishing them openly or placing them in a model's recursive input risks task exposure and prevents any later claim they were unseen. Keep full output in access-controlled private provenance if retained; future model-facing loaders must be explicitly restricted to the reviewed task inputs and must exclude all witness/signature/selection files. Batch index and manifests include case IDs and lineage labels, while wrappers/records may reveal local absolute paths and usernames: inspect and sanitize for any public upload. Hashes are integrity identifiers, not secrecy guarantees for guessable content. Do not upload model weights, tokens, credentials, SSH details or private local paths in a public report. Actual publication requires separately checked project policy and destination; no upload was performed here.

All conclusions remain bounded by unauthenticated same-user filesystem custody, unverified clock/process observations, unobserved transitive effects and lack of runtime parser/engine validation. A separate reviewed implementation, exact source/data bindings, budget, runtime audit and stop rules are required for any later feasibility call. This report grants no such execution.
