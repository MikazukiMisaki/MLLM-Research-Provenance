# Review of proposed task support and custody

Decision: **useful design draft; do not freeze as written**. The draft appropriately limits the existing authored case to development, requires unknown historical/pretraining exposure to remain unknown, and does not represent a second agent using the same account as an independent custody boundary. Without an independently administered boundary, a reproducible shared-access feasibility study may be exploratory but is not a blinded confirmatory study. Commitments can bind a later opening; they cannot prove that nobody previously accessed a task or that the submitted manifest is complete.

**Necessary correction to the overlap rule:** the proposed fixed target explicitly contains the same two task families in every partition. Mere common membership in a family, use of a general public grammar, or a shared abstract family template is not automatically a leakage or task-identity conflict. Interpreting every shared template/ancestor as prohibited would make the proposed within-family train/evaluation split impossible, or would silently change its estimand to unseen-template generalization. Freeze a typed lineage graph distinguishing common domain/grammar/family ancestry from concrete task derivation, paraphrase, direct descendant, and predeclared near-duplicate relations. Prohibit the latter across partitions, record ambiguous cases as UNKNOWN, and perform a separately specified within-family split. If the intended claim instead concerns unseen templates or families, define a different split and target with sufficient independent templates or families; do not infer that claim from task-level disjointness.

The proposed per-family support floor is only a practical screening threshold. Ten distinct tasks per family do not justify broad ALFWorld generalization. Sampling and weighting should reflect the frozen target and valid accepted support. Any potentially revealing aggregate or commitment scheme requires a concrete threat model, minimum disclosure threshold, and access policy; keyed commitments and self-reported role separation alone do not prove custody. Unknown overlap with protected or pretraining material remains unknown, and no such material should be opened to resolve it.

The procedure remains a draft: actual support generation, semantic near-duplicate handling, source/correction provenance, independent custody, complete numeric budgets, real training checkpoints and the runnable three-arm implementation remain unestablished. Engineering and development checks can continue without inventing confirmation evidence. No tasks, trajectories, seeds, model or environment were opened or run for this review.
