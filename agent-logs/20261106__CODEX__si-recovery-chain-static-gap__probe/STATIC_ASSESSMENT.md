## What remains unproven or missing for a new bounded case

- The most recent clean-1 CPU witness is a genuinely observed *scripted* self-authored path with a goal and six environment steps; it did not load P0, invoke either recovery model stage or demonstrate a model-selected action. Earlier one-action probe uses a distinct self-authored input and was only a draft; do not conflate the two cases or infer that either ran a two-stage chain.
- The existing H4 operator constructor expects `QwenPolicy._config`; SFTP0QwenPolicy wraps a backend and does not present that direct interface. Existing R4 uses a separate unadapted recovery model and an SFT-P0 continuation, not a demonstrated single P0 LoRA model running both stages. Decide and preregister the intended treatment before wiring; do not silently swap model/adapter or reuse historical H4 results as P0 proof. Two simultaneously loaded 8B backends are a specific old implementation choice and cannot be assumed to fit the shared GPU.
- An isolated, exact-byte new development-only owner/worker for one self-authored recovery state is not established by this source review. It needs explicit public observation/history/valid-actions construction, two stage-specific raw generations, independent parsers, stage-invalid and malformed/truncated failure handling, exact unique environment grounding, at most one permitted step, distinct append-only evidence and complete operation/token/device accounting. It must not scan old pools, derive new cases from protected trajectories, repair semantic content, or hide UNKNOWN started-but-unreturned operations. The two-stage implementation's stage-2-after-stage-1-failure behavior must be explicitly accounted for; changing it alters the frozen treatment and needs a separate protocol decision.
- A direct PddlEnv development witness or successful parser-only diagnostic does not establish equivalence to the normal ALFWorld wrapper, nor that a self-authored case is a scientifically independent evaluation distribution. One early feasibility case cannot supply success-rate or precision evidence.
- Need source, runtime and exact model/adapter identity; effective input token IDs and tokenizer/decoder binding; a verified clean offline environment; a resource plan covering model/adapter load and at most two generations, environment construct/load/reset/step/close, CPU/GPU and helper operations, started-unknown, wall-time/cleanup tails; and a bounded parent/child recorder independently reviewed under shared-GPU conditions. Past GPU connectivity or an idle snapshot is not a model-fit result. Existing one-shot claims must not be reused.
- For later self-improvement claims, independently sourced, lineage-audited development/acquisition and confirmation tasks, prespecified paired comparison, statistical precision and complete training/evaluation budget remain separate scientific prerequisites. SI-U1 was inconclusive and does not establish a benefit; do not merge it with this engineering witness.
