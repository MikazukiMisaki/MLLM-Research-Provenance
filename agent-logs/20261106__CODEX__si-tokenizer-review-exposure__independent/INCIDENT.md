The file was a production source dependency explicitly bound by the candidate, but the review should have restricted printed source lines to the import statements and specific prompt functions. Subsequent source inspection is limited to relevant minimal sections and known self-authored inputs. Source hashing does not imply that historical task custody or non-exposure is established. This record does not claim that the exposure affected, or could not affect, future scientific independence; the limitation is retained for the coordinator to assess.
