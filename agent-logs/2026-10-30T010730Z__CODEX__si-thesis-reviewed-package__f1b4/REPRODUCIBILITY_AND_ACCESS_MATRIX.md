## What can be claimed and what remains

1. **Arithmetic audit:** Previous independently recorded computation reproduced counts, contrasts, p-values and intervals from the approved extracted analysis. It supports auditability of those calculations given controlled-access records and code; it is not a model/environment rerun or proof of independent sample validity.
2. **Forensic analysis of actual outputs:** Local evidence and most referenced materials are present and hash-bound as described. A fresh approved analysis would still need proper access and must explicitly retain both the original failure and the post-evaluation extractor supplement. This check does not run such an analysis.
3. **Training/evaluation re-execution:** Current local input bytes being present is necessary but insufficient. Establish all exact training dataset members and lineage, runtime dependencies including ALFWorld/TextWorld and system/GPU details, checkpoint access and licenses, configuration and seeds, independent scheduling/authorization, budget, and actual validation. Exact numerical replication is not guaranteed even with byte-identical inputs. No claim is made that this study has been rerun, or can be rerun elsewhere.
4. **Scientific generalization:** 82 TRAIN parents were compared using one trained seed for each continuation. Historical exposure, identity/scene independence and population-sampling assumptions remain unresolved; more complete local file hashes cannot remedy those epistemic gaps.
5. **Public material:** Aggregate findings and the manuscript may be proposed for external sharing after research-owner, privacy, licensing and redaction review; protected trajectories, task identities, prompts, model/adapter weights, environment paths and datasets remain controlled-access. Hashes can themselves enable linkage and should be reviewed. The existence of a private provenance repository does not grant public redistribution rights.
