# Product adjudication provenance

The original adjudication and its later append-only addendum are preserved as separate exact-byte records. The independent review supports the recorded `INNER_COMPLETED_OUTER_UNKNOWN_NO_RETRY` classification and conditional development eligibility without converting the external guardian observation failure into PASS. The addendum explicitly records `evaluation_authorized=false`: eligibility does not authorize an evaluator, experiment, training retry, held-out inference or an efficacy claim. Any future concrete execution remains subject to its own complete independent review and execution conditions.

Only sanitized, path-free status documents and hashes are published. Original training rows, raw machine paths, process details, authorization and model weights are not included.
