Full ledgers, fixtures, subprocess stderr and parent chain are under `synthetic_evidence/`. The terminal result `won=true` is a **child-claimed fictional result**, not a native or physical success. The fake parent budget totals for the positive fixture are 4 materializations, 8 each of construct/init/reset/model load/generation/step, plus conservative reservations of 8 × (4096 input + 16 output) tokens, with no refunds. These are simulated effects and reservations, not observed actual resource use.
