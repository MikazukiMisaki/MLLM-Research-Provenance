# From one development episode to self-improvement: independent scientific review

None of the numeric sample sizes below establishes an available task pool, budget approval, new result, or authorization to execute. ## Existing evidence and the question worth answering

SI-U1 evaluated fixed checkpoints on 82 distinct parents from TRAIN: P0 65/82, expert continuation 68/82, self-success continuation 67/82; the two predeclared comparisons had Holm-adjusted p=1 and the accepted result was INCONCLUSIVE. Parent-level historical/pretraining exposure is not established. R19's 32/36 oracle versus 30/36 best fixed K is a 5.56-point post-hoc upper envelope, not an implementable gain or an effect-size estimate for this proposed comparison. See source hashes below.

One defensible next question is whether a **frozen learned policy** trained on carefully validated agent-recovery experience improves terminal task success against P0 and against an equal-dose continuation trained on the same acquisition framework's non-recovery success pool. The intervention-only A/B/C draft does not train an updated policy and by itself cannot answer this question. A difference between trained arms remains an operational comparison: recovery-derived states and ordinary success states may have different distributions; equal supervised dose does not identify a pure data-source causal effect.

## Concrete staged proposal — deliberately conditional

1. **Engineering only.** Independently repair/review exact P0 source and execute at most the separately scoped one H50 development episode when its prerequisites are met. It can measure actual model loading, prompts, parser behavior, step costs and completed/UNKNOWN status. The already scripted case has been exposed; outcome is not a test result. No experiment in this note implies or overrides separate run boundaries.
2. **Four-group development recovery feasibility.** The existing unfrozen pre-registration draft describes up to four distinct predeclared development groups, shared prefix, P0 A / recovery B / stage-1-text-masked C. It remains unimplemented as an accepted complete real pipeline; four eligible independent groups have not been shown. The stage-1 valid bit is retained in C; A has a different call budget. No source replacement, no retry. If all four do not qualify, say insufficient feasibility; even 4/4 has no efficacy interpretation. This stage tests execution and plausibility, not self-improvement.
3. **Construct and lock training evidence, without opening protected task contents.** Use only fresh self-authored tasks from a reviewed, versioned generator. Log generator inputs, source, actor/model/adapter, raw outputs, states, grounding, action map, terminal outcome and all invalid/UNKNOWN attempts. Freeze a common acquisition pool, selection rules, deduplication and quota before comparing outcomes. A possible operational floor, not an already available resource: 20 distinct eligible training task components per arm, one reviewed state/action target per component, with matched declared strata and no shared exact task/ancestor across groups; stop if insufficient. Validate that each target action is actually applicable in its recorded pre-decision state and provenance/semantic quality is reviewed. A recovered final success does not by itself prove an earlier action is a correct learning target. Explicitly label human/planner-authored targets as such; do not present them as autonomous experience. Never coerce invalid, unknown, failed, scripted or duplicate material into positive self-generated labels. Match and disclose attempts, quality and selection; intrinsically different states or failed acquisition must not be concealed as identical data. Freeze extraction and filtering before model outcomes and prevent evaluation data entering selection, target formation, hyperparameter choice or model training.
4. **Two tightly matched exploratory continuation arms.** Example minimum: from identical frozen P0, B gets one validated normal-success action target per each of 20 distinct training components; C gets one validated recovery-derived target per each of 20 other qualifying components, same frozen prompt, label mask, optimizer/seed, four epochs, batch 1, accumulation 4 and checkpoint rule, for 80 presentations and 20 updates per arm if exactly 20 valid rows each. Equalize target-token dose explicitly or disclose failure to match; never pad labels to fabricate an actual target. If 4096 total input tokens and 16 supervised tokens per row are enforced, those 160 presentations have <=655,360 total training input tokens including <=2,560 supervised target tokens (not additive). The example is a feasibility floor, not a validated recipe, sufficient effective sample size, reliable training estimate, or authorization. If no such pool or training resource bound exists, stop at engineering evidence instead of pretending 20 are available.
5. **Evaluation only on separately frozen supports and a prespecified estimand.** To isolate update effects evaluate A=P0, B=normal-success SFT and C=recovery-derived SFT on the same independently sampled task/seed per triplet and prespecified arm order, H50. Freeze policy checkpoints, primary `C−A` and `C−B`, endpoint, invalid classification, missing-data STOP, randomization and multiplicity before any outcomes. Normal model invalid can be prespecified as a non-success, but infrastructure error or incomplete outcome is UNKNOWN, stops the study and is not imputed. For a pilot, report all outcomes and counts descriptively; freeze a fresh independent confirmation protocol and support if pilot findings change any design. Do not reuse exposed development tasks as confirmatory.

Make a frozen manifest of generator revision and source, role graph, goal/operator templates, initial state factors, seeds and immutable hashes before generation; preserve all candidate draws and outcome-independent rejection causes. If custody, separation or selection restrictions cannot be demonstrated, evaluation is explicitly shared-access exploratory, not held-out. Do not claim transfer to new families or the official benchmark from these examples.

## Precision: two explicit options and what they mean

For a possible **fixed-checkpoint, fixed constructed distribution** estimand with IID paired draws *with replacement*, the outcome difference D is in [-1,1]. For **two** simultaneously reported prespecified comparisons, Bonferroni plus the two-sided Hoeffding inequality gives simultaneous 95% radius `sqrt(2 ln(80)/n)` around each paired mean; clip endpoints to [-1,1]. This conservative design bound is neither a sample-size power analysis nor a claim that the required IID support currently exists. It counts paired draws, not three episodes per draw or repeated steps; neither common templates nor sampling with replacement guarantees independently generated draws unless audited. A deterministic finite support's exact weighted effect can instead be obtained by evaluating its full support once under its own frozen design; that does not estimate performance on new families or across training seeds.

| Paired draws, 3 arms | 95% simultaneous distribution-free radius | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| 20 | ±66.20 percentage points | engineering/pilot only; cannot establish meaningful gain or safety |
| 100 | ±29.60 points | still too imprecise for a 5–10 point effect |
| 877 | ±10.00 points | bound on precision only, potentially extremely expensive |

For a mean estimate exactly +10 points at n=100, the conservative interval is approximately [-19.60, +39.60] points before clipping. This illustration is synthetic, not observed. Intervals can be narrower with separately prespecified variance-sensitive valid methods, but no precision or power guarantee follows from unknown discordance. At n=20 there is insufficient inferential precision even if all execution succeeds. A >5-point lower-bound decision is a stronger hypothesis than detecting benefit greater than zero: at a true effect exactly +5 points a high passing probability conflicts with false-positive control. Do not use the R19 oracle gap as an expected training effect; no across-training-seed variance is estimable from a single C/B run.
