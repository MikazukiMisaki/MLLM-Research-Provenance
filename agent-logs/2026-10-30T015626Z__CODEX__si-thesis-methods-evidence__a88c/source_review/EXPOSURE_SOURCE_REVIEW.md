## Evidence boundaries and rights

Further only-read-only evidence worth locating is contemporaneous input-version/installation and generator receipts, a fuller inventory of actual historical runs and candidate processing, precise loaded base model/tokenizer revision, and authoritative archive-specific use terms. Such receipts must be assessed for completeness and identity independently; the absence of a receipt is not a negative exposure record. Even an otherwise verified model snapshot cannot establish source exclusion without adequate model corpus provenance. The [repository software license](https://github.com/alfworld/alfworld/blob/aaba6870f86c5be6a08a491f32a50b906227bc3e/LICENSE) and model license do not by themselves settle separately distributed task archive rights. The existing open [archive-terms issue](https://github.com/alfworld/alfworld/issues/132) is a request for clarification, not a legal ruling.

No candidate task/game content or evaluation result was inspected or copied. No new task archive, model, CUDA, environment, task generation, evaluation or training was run, and no scientific or original evidence file was edited. `METADATA_CHECK.json` records before/after checksums of all eight direct local inputs. API metadata and this review are new separate evidence only.
