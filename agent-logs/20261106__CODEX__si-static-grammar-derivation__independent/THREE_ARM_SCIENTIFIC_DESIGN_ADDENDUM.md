# Three-arm scientific design addendum

Status: design for independent review; not an authorization, a frozen protocol, or a scientific result.

The research question is whether self-improvement improves agent success relative to the original P0 policy. A C4-versus-C1 contrast alone cannot answer that question. On the same independently sampled, frozen, self-authored evaluation tasks, evaluate the fixed P0, C1, and C4 checkpoints with identical interface and per-task limits. Predeclare C4 minus P0 as the main efficacy contrast and C4 minus C1 as the comparison of the two experience-weighting conditions; treat C1 minus P0 as descriptive unless it is included in the multiplicity plan. Shared episodes yield paired binary differences, and invalid outputs, timeouts, and technical interruptions require prespecified separate handling; do not silently discard or relabel unknown outcomes.

Construct training and evaluation tasks from distinct frozen lineage, using training-only P0 observations to obtain and verify corrected successes. Keep collection and preprocessing common between C1 and C4, varying only the prespecified experience weighting. Test tasks must not influence training, repair, filtering, or checkpoint selection. Freeze the task generator version, distribution and weights, rejection/eligibility rules, per-draw private RNG, resulting lineage, and checkpoints before opening evaluation outcomes. Generator process-global randomness and feasibility checks must not silently couple otherwise claimed independent draws. Independent review must first establish an implementable task-identity and custody design; historical and pretraining exposure remains unknown.

Do not conflate the previous prototype's target task family with a newly observed failure type. If weighting is based on error type, predefine sample-level attribution, supporting evidence, multiple-label handling and counts for the new pool; otherwise define the actual weighting criterion without implying error-specific correction. Label whether each successful correction came from P0 itself, an external model, a human, a planner, or another source. A correction from outside P0 is not a P0 self-generated success. The existing C4 weighted-training code was verified only on synthetic data and must not be represented as an implemented real-data training pipeline.

Scope of inference is the stipulated distribution of two fixed task families and the evaluated fixed checkpoints. Even with independent draws it does not establish generalization across new task families, across training seeds, or freedom from unknown historical or pretraining exposure. Training, failure recovery, and deployment costs must be measured separately; the checkpoint and training recipe are not yet ready for formal execution.

For two prespecified contrasts, 877 independently sampled paired tasks is a conservative sufficient sample for simultaneous 95% distribution-free Hoeffding intervals with half-width at most 10 percentage points. It is not a guarantee of 80% power or of detecting a 10-point true effect. The number assumes genuine independent draws from the stated distribution, not 877 correlated variants masquerading as independent families. A 20-task pilot tests feasibility only, cannot establish efficacy, and any adaptive selection using pilot outcomes requires a fresh confirmatory sample or a predeclared valid sequential procedure.

Conditional planning ceilings, not a complete budget: 50 generations and 1 reset plus 50 steps per arm per task give three-arm 20-task evaluation at most 3,000 generations and 3,060 environment operations. Adding the earlier conditional acquisition ceiling of 2,000 generations and 2,530 operations yields 5,000 and 5,590, excluding training and failed or unobserved work. At 877 evaluation tasks, three arms have ceilings 131,550 generations and 134,181 operations; plus that acquisition allowance gives 133,550 and 136,711, again excluding training, retries, resource failures and GPU time. These deliberately pessimistic limits must not be confused with an approved expenditure or an estimate of actual use.

Stop before efficacy evaluation if task provenance, lineage isolation, common data construction, fixed checkpoints, per-arm accounting, prespecified uncertainty or complete resource budget cannot be established. Current model-free engineering checks and synthetic fixtures are not efficacy evidence.
