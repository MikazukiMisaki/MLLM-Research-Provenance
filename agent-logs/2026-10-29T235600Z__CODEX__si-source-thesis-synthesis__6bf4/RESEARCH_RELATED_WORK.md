# SI-U1 / R19 related-work reading notes

Status: targeted primary-paper reading for a thesis draft, not an exhaustive literature review and not a finding of proven novelty. Distinguish a paper's reported setting and outcomes from SI-U1: task pools, initial checkpoints, training data, sample selection, implementation and evaluation differ, so success percentages cannot be compared as a controlled head-to-head result.

## Benchmark and model references

1. **Shridhar, Yuan, Côté, Bisk, Trischler and Hausknecht, “ALFWorld: Aligning Text and Embodied Environments for Interactive Learning,” 2021.** [Official paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.03768); [paper full text, §4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2010.03768). The paper introduces linked abstract text and embodied task environments and studies, among other questions, whether interactive text-world training transfers to embodied settings. Its §4.1 compares interactive DAgger training with behavior cloning from expert demonstrations. SI-U1 uses only the text-based environment, not an embodied-transfer evaluation; its fixed TRAIN-split schedule and incompletely known exposure history must not be described as the paper's held-out embodied evaluation or general ALFWorld performance. ALFWorld and expert-vs-interactive comparisons are prior work, not innovations introduced here.

2. **Yang et al., “Qwen3 Technical Report,” 2025.** [Official paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.09388); [full text, §4.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.09388). The report describes the Qwen3 model family and training, including the fused thinking/non-thinking interface. SI-U1 uses the Qwen3-8B model in an action-only, thinking-disabled configuration and starts from an additional expert-supervised P0 adapter; that specific project checkpoint and prompt are experiment artifacts, not claims made by the Qwen3 report. Qwen3's upstream data exposure is not established by the paper at the level needed to certify these tasks as unseen.

## Directly related self-generated agent experience

3. **Dou, Yang, Wu, Chang and Peng, “Re-ReST: Reflection-Reinforced Self-Training for Language Agents,” EMNLP 2024, pp. 15394–15411.** [Official ACL record](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.861/); [paper PDF, §2 and §3.2, Tables 1–2](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.861.pdf). The paper describes language-agent self-training on selected self-generated thought/action trajectories and adds a separate reflector using external feedback to refine low-quality samples. In its ALFWorld setup it uses Llama-2-7B, a binary success indicator and LoRA; the authors report evaluation on 134 unseen environments and include a plain self-training comparison. Thus successful self-generated agent trajectory training in ALFWorld and using external success feedback both precede SI-U1. SI-U1 differs in using action-only samples from a frozen expert-initialized Qwen3-8B P0, no reflector in the continuation comparison, and a same-P0 expert-replay arm matched on family-level training-parent counts and supervision dose. These differences describe a research configuration, not by themselves a proven methodological innovation or an isolated source effect; SI-U1's B/C states and tasks also differ, and it does not use a certified unseen evaluation set. Paper-reported results should be cited in their original setting, not compared numerically to our 82 tasks as if directly matched.

4. **Sarukkai, Xie and Fatahalian, “Self-Generated In-Context Examples Improve LLM Agents for Sequential Decision-Making Tasks,” arXiv:2505.00234v3, 2025.** [Official arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00234); [full text, §§3, 5–6 and Appendix C](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.00234). The main method builds and curates a database of an agent's successful trajectories for in-context use, with a small set of human-generated initial examples assumed. Crucially, Appendix C also explicitly fine-tunes GPT-4o-mini on the self-collected, curated database in a ReAct-style format and reports ALFWorld performance alongside its in-context method. Therefore it would be misleading to say the prior paper is only prompting, or that SI-U1 first trains an ALFWorld agent on its own successful trajectories. SI-U1 instead uses a single expert-initialized Qwen3-8B starting checkpoint, fixed P0 action interface and a B/C matched-supervision expert-versus-self continuation comparison with a paired 82-task result. The sources, training procedure and target examples remain confounded with task and state distribution; neither this comparison nor the cited paper alone establishes that such matched controls are novel or that C improves P0.

## Thesis-positioning implications

- Describe the feasible contribution as a **controlled, reproducible empirical comparison and failure analysis** under a clearly specified, limited budget and starting checkpoint. Do not claim that self-training successful agent trajectories, using ALFWorld, or selecting success feedback is a new algorithmic invention.
- The explicit shared-P0, expert replay and self-success continuation conditions, predeclared paired evaluation, logging/cost accounting and diagnostic evidence can make a useful master's thesis **study design**, but a claim of unique novelty requires a broader systematic related-work review; existing two studies already overlap materially with the central method.
- SI-U1 remains inconclusive: C 67/82 versus A 65/82 and B 68/82 with Holm-adjusted p values 1.00. It has a single training seed per continuation and fixed TRAIN-partition tasks, with historical scene exposure incompletely known. The prior papers' reported results do not establish superiority or impossibility for this separate configuration.
- R19's 32/36 hindsight envelope among tested K values is not a deployable adaptive-stopping policy and does not establish success-rate improvement for SI-U1. Related work on recovery, reflection and adaptive stopping needs a separate source-level comparison if that material becomes a thesis contribution.
- Before submission, independently verify publication metadata and bibliography style against the institution's rules, add primary citations at the relevant thesis claims, and make the method/data/evidence appendix shareable without disclosing private task IDs or trajectory contents.
