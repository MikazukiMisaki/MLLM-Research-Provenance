# Same-environment task support: proposed extension and limits

**Decision: expand development tooling, not claim an independent 40+20-task support or start a scientific evaluation.** The two concrete extensions below use the existing published ALFWorld PDDL action model. They are unimplemented designs, not generated or demonstrated cases. A task solution in this PDDL is a symbolic claim whose engine, instruction, grounding and wrapper behavior must still be tested. This review read only explicitly self-authored generator source, prior design/reviews, and public domain action definitions; no historical/protected task, trajectory, schedule or seed was accessed and no model, environment, GPU or generator was run.

## Existing support and what counts

The CLEAN development constructor (`generate_development.py`, SHA256 `3c3aea3d740e26eda2d4810bbcac8be09b4bd9a77cee317935a8f906a7d1e90e`) covers two binary structural factors: source open/closed and actor initially at source/sink. It has at most four role-based combinations; the already separately witnessed original case is excluded from new generation by its registry. Only one clean case has a prior individually audited CPU witness; the other configurations do not have established engine validity. Names or paraphrases do not multiply the support. Its `isClean(object)` goal does not establish generalization to arbitrary cleaning tasks.

The separate transport generator (`generate.py`, SHA256 `d4af2b0a5c6963efeebec152a076b1bcd332e88876ba8e83e50e539509bea7c3`) has 2 × 2 × 3 = 12 factor combinations, all with the same placement goal and shared template. It marks them static, unvalidated drafts, and its default instruction is `UNKNOWN GOAL`. The toy train/evaluation tags have no custody meaning. Four CLEAN combinations plus 12 transport tuples are neither 40 distinct evaluation-support units nor an extra 20 independent collection/development units; structural groups may be fewer than tuples, cross-family shared paths can create further dependencies, and distinct canonical hashes alone do not establish statistical independence or unknown historical exposure. The proposed 40+20 is a planning floor—20 train/collection plus 20 feasibility plus 20 confirmation, balanced 10 per family in each subset—not a power calculation, existing allocation, or approval for a confirmatory claim.

## Two additional semantics in the same domain

Both candidate classes extend the task goal, not the model, prompt, decoding, frozen intervention or environment implementation. They need new reviewed constructor, task-language compiler, independent checker and runtime integration. Pin the existing public domain (`alfred.pddl` SHA256 `f1473041ed01da514de1f87adfd7ef0804862361f83ccf63c75e43633b0eb280`) and grammar (`alfred.twl2` SHA256 `c5b22f0f73309a7fd7c8e5b71de42ad4803b60766ab4a51cc2feb34162be036b`), allowing only explicitly reviewed task-specific instruction rendering. No new simulator is introduced.

1. **Clean then deliver.** Goal is `(and (isClean target) (inReceptacle target destination))`, with a unique cleanable, pickupable target initially in a source at a different location from destination. A truthful instruction is “Clean the [uniquely identified target] and put it in the [uniquely identified destination].” The static domain permits source opening if required, pickup with empty hand, navigation to a `SinkBasinType` receptacle, `CleanObject` while holding target, navigation to destination, opening if needed, and `PutObject` with matching `objectType`, `receptacleType` and `canContain`. Both conjuncts must be checked: clean-only or delivery-only is failure.
2. **Heat then deliver.** Goal is `(and (isHot target) (inReceptacle target destination))`, with a unique heatable pickupable target, a microwave of `MicrowaveType`, and corresponding truthful instruction. The existing `HeatObject` requires the actor at the appliance and holding the heatable target, adds `isHot` and removes `isCool`; the delivery preconditions are as above. Do not import physical assumptions absent from the pinned domain: its HeatObject preconditions do not include opening or closing the microwave, so whether rendered action/engine requires more is UNKNOWN until tested. Verify no later action invalidates either goal conjunct. The intent is a different processing action and goal, not an assertion of independent sampling or an unseen-family generalization result.

In both cases `GotoLocation` requires a receptacle at the destination location, `OpenObject` requires an openable closed receptacle co-located with the actor, and `PickupObject` requires empty hand and accessible source. Ensure consistent actor/object location, disjoint source/destination, compatible destination contents, no contradictory facts, target not already delivered or processed, and truthful unambiguous object and destination identifiers. If process device and source or destination are co-located, do not add a fake navigation step; if device equals destination, ensure its actual declared type and containment relation satisfy both processing and put actions. The pinned PDDL allows symbolic co-location; actual supported grammar, object types, game rendering, feasibility and wrapper equivalence are not yet established.

## Explicit candidate arithmetic, not validated support

An intentionally finite starting parameter space for **each** new class has: three location equivalence patterns (source/process/destination all different, source=process != destination, or source != process=destination); two actor starts (source vs destination, always distinct); two source door states; two destination door states; and two distractor conditions (none vs one uniquely named, non-target object). Thus **3 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 48 proposed parameter tuples per class**, 96 across two classes. These are proposed configurations, not 96 verified or independent tasks. No case is actually constructed by this arithmetic. It assumes both source and destination can be openable under each eligible typed configuration; type restrictions, device/destination overlap, ambiguity, invalid grammar, initial goal, horizon and planner or engine failures can reduce this number, potentially to zero. Adding a distractor or changing a door state alone must not be sold as independent evidence.

A pre-specified conservative *illustrative* split-group rule that removes both door bits and the distractor from identity and groups by family, location pattern and actor start yields **at most 3 × 2 = 6 groups per family**, assuming no additional merges. If the two classes have the same canonical goal-blind setup/common action subpath, the required cross-family edges can merge groups further. Under that rule the candidate design cannot meet a requirement of 10 disjoint groups per family in each of collection, feasibility and confirmation (30 per family); no amount of identifier renaming fixes this. A stricter transitive one-factor-neighbor rule can collapse even more; a different predeclared scientifically justified rule gives a different count. Group count is an upper bound, not proof of statistically independent tasks, natural task distribution or 40+20 validity.

To increase *potential* support, design multiple independently authored, materially different scene schemas and causal dependency structures, and demonstrate their actual number of accepted non-overlapping groups by the frozen algorithm. Examples include distractors requiring a genuinely different search dependency, a separately gated source or delivery stage, or different dependency order where the operator semantics truly allow it. Such variations require specifying the full role graph, valid action path, actionability, truthful language, required budget and overlap rule **before** model results; adding names, text paraphrases, or two kinds of trivial door bit cannot be counted as new independent scenes. Authors must record shared ancestry; do not claim independence simply by using a different constructor. Until enough accepted groups and custody exist, either run a clearly labelled exploratory within-family study using its actual group count or stop the proposed independent 40+20 study. Do not modify the target count after seeing outcomes to manufacture PASS.

## Isolated construction, de-duplication and validation

1. Freeze task schemas, candidate population, rejection constraints, grouping/signature algorithm, treatment assignments, task exposure policy and feasible-support target before any policy outcomes. Distinguish a general public domain/family ancestor (permitted across partitions) from an actual concrete case derivation, shared underlying instance, paraphrase, or a predeclared near duplicate. Derive signatures from independently validated raw graphs and actions, rather than accepting self-reported digest strings.
2. Use an independently administered custodian to generate separate collection, feasibility and confirmation materials where possible. Have them assign *whole transitive connected components* after exact role-preserving canonicalization and declared near-duplicate/cross-family edges, before distributing any case or obtaining outcomes. Freeze partitions and retain all rejected/unknown instances, collision decisions and manifests. If the same coordinator can inspect every partition, call the work exploratory/shared-access; a hash, commitment or another agent under the same account does not prove blinding or no prior exposure. Protected or pretraining overlap is UNKNOWN without an authorized scoped custodian/reference; do not open the protected material to fill this gap. A nonmatch is not proof of non-exposure. An unknown or ambiguous conflict cannot enter a claimed independent support.
3. Build a separate bounded static checker directly from pinned typed preconditions/effects, not merely replaying generator-supplied expected strings. Verify initial nonterminal goal, unique target and required typed objects, valid one-holder/world-state consistency, supported operators, every action precondition/effect, both final predicates and path length ≤ frozen horizon. Include adversarial tests: ambiguous labels, missing cleanable/heatable, wrong device type, closed inaccessible source/destination, missing `canContain`, initial goal, conflicting facts, wrong conjunct, incorrect action/step count and cyclic duplicate groups. Reject unsupported or unknown semantics, not repair-and-silently-replace.
4. Check each instruction against the actual goal and unique actionable references: a separately validated structured goal-to-language mapping, independent reverse parse or explicit human review where parser coverage is incomplete, then compare intended vs runtime-rendered instruction and terminal goal. Check positive and negative feedback, intermediate and terminal state facts, exact grounded command mappings and alternatives. Any mismatch or ambiguity is FAIL/UNKNOWN. Preserve the rejected candidate and reason. A truthful synthetic static witness cannot stand in for an actual engine observation.
5. Only as a separate, bounded CPU engineering validation phase, run at most one witnessed attempt per exact candidate, with successful parse/compile, construct/load/reset, full scripted solution ≤8 environment steps and goal success; early-stop on mismatch, crash, timeout or missing receipt. Do not imply a direct-engine witness proves the production wrapper; compare the adapter/wrapper explicitly before relying on it. No model, policy generation, CUDA or training needed for this task-validity phase. Static rejection and failed/partial operations remain in the ledger and no automatic retries.

For **V** accepted-for-witness candidates, the nominal upper ceiling is V constructs, V loads, V resets and 8V step starts, or ≤9V reset-plus-step starts; 60 such witnesses would be ≤60 resets and ≤480 action-step starts (≤540 combined), excluding compile/translation work, rejected candidates, actual CPU/peak memory/time, setup/cleanup and any interrupted external operation. The entire illustrative 96-tuple design would cap one witness each at 96 resets and 768 step starts (864 combined), plus the same exclusions. These are proposed outer caps, not actual costs or permission to execute; source and process placement can lower steps, while hidden engine work is UNKNOWN. Specify separate bounded counts for all proposed/rejected candidates, compile/translation attempts, CPU seconds, memory and storage before execution; no complete physical budget exists yet.

## Minimum acceptance and stop

A candidate supports development only if static checks and its individually bound real engine witness succeed with unambiguous instruction/grounding and full goal state. A candidate supports a claimed independent partition only if provenance/custody and no-disallowed-overlap have passed the frozen procedure; unknown is not a pass. The 40+20 design can proceed as such only after **at least 10 admissible whole groups per family in each of the three partitions**, with independent custody for a blinded claim, completed task-validation records, a frozen analysis and full acquisition/training/three-arm evaluation/failure-tail budget. Counts alone never establish statistical power or independence. Stop the affected stage on insufficient groups, impossible goal, ambiguity, overlap UNKNOWN, missing custody, task/grammar drift, mismatched outcome, exhausted validation budget or incomplete external operation; preserve original evidence and do not replace failures selectively. A weaker exploratory study may be separately specified and honestly labelled, with any self-improvement claim bounded to its observed task support.
