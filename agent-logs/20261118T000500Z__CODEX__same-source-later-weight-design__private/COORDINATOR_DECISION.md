# Prospective exploratory decision: same-success-trace later-step weighting

The preceding low-learning-rate continuation failed its frozen exploratory screen. The separate within-parent duplicate-state treatment is also dropped: its predeclared check found 99 distinct proxy states across 99 rows, and every weight was 1. Neither prior run is resumed or relabeled.

Candidate next investigation: initialize both trained arms from the same completed C-quarter adapter, use the same 99 logged decisions from its ten successful development parents, same data order and tokenized inputs, optimizer, training seed, 396 microbatches and 33 updates. A uses weight 1, B uses w_j=0.5+(j+1)/(n+1), with j zero-based in a parent trajectory of n decisions. This prespecified mild weighting has parent row-weight sum n and range strictly between 0.5 and 1.5. It is chosen to explore emphasis on later actions while avoiding extreme weights. Each arm uses the same completion-token mean per example and mean over 12 microbatches. A third arm is the uncontinued C-quarter. No action is assumed correct or optimal merely because the full trajectory succeeded. Later position is not evidence of actual semantic progress or cause of success.

This is an adaptive, exploratory development hypothesis chosen after earlier results; its purpose is a small controlled signal, not independent confirmation or proof of general improvement. Same example and row-weight totals do not establish equal task-wise weighted token mass or gradients. The re-created chat tokens will be new training encoding, not claimed to equal unrecorded historical token IDs. Uniform per-example loss may differ from historical token-normalized objectives; both training arms must use the same definition.

Before any training: verify actual logged action and transition alignment, source and starting adapter identity, absence of samples from evaluation parents, rendering, label mask and all sequence lengths, software implementation and minimum control tests; bind exact code/config/dataset and training/validation budget. Before evaluation: freeze separate DEV parents, pair ordering, success/unknown handling, resource ceilings and screening criteria, without inspecting their outcomes. All existing physical costs remain in the ledger, and a new scope and absolute wall deadline must be stated prospectively. No automatic retries, silent replacement, or turning UNKNOWN into failure. This decision authorizes implementation and CPU testing only; it is not a claim that either training or evaluation ran or is ready.
