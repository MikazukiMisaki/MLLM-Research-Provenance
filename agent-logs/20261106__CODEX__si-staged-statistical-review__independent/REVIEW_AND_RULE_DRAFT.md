# Independent statistical review and fixed-sample decision-rule draft

Status `PLANNING_ONLY_NOT_PREREGISTERED_OR_EXECUTABLE_AS_A_RESEARCH_RUN`. No protected tasks, model, CUDA or environment were accessed. The rule below is a concrete candidate for advance review, not a declaration that its hypotheses, data, computational budget or treatment implementations currently exist. The accompanying pure function evaluates complete synthetic binary triplets only and cannot certify task custody, real execution or randomization.

## Mathematical review and a substantive design mismatch

The staged-rule draft and token-budget table have correct conditional arithmetic. For two prespecified paired differences in `[-1,1]`, simultaneous two-sided 95% Hoeffding intervals using Bonferroni have radius `sqrt(2 ln(80)/n)`: at `n=3506`, approximately **0.0499973**. The three-arm H50 evaluation ceiling is 10,518 episodes, 525,900 generations and 536,418 reset-plus-step operations; adding the separate illustrative 2,000-generation/2,530-operation acquisition envelope yields 527,900/538,948. At a hypothetical enforced 4,096 input/64 output tokens per call, this last total gives 2,162,278,400 input and 33,785,600 output tokens. The drafts also correctly identify that training, task validation, failed/open work, hardware and storage remain extra and currently unknown. These are bounds under specific proposed contracts, not measured consumption or a full approved budget.

**The lower-confidence-bound rule is a poor design if the intended aim is to detect a true 5-percentage-point improvement.** At this sample size, the candidate rule `LCB(C4−P0) > 0.05` requires an observed net advantage of at least 351/3506, approximately 10.01 points. If the true effect is exactly 5 points, that value is the boundary of the null for the stronger claim `effect > 5 points`; the one-sided Hoeffding bound for incorrectly passing this rule is at most `exp(-n*r²/2) = 1/80 = 1.25%`. This is a type-I guarantee at the boundary, not 98.75% power. A high probability of declaring `effect > 5 points` when the truth is exactly 5 points is incompatible with such error control. If the actual goal is instead detecting a nonzero benefit when the truth is 5 points, predeclare a zero-effect hypothesis and calculate power for an assumed paired-discordance distribution; do not relabel an `LCB > 5 points` rule. A precision bound alone is not a power calculation.

For reference, even a true effect appreciably above 5 points requires a credible prospective discordance/variance model and a power calculation for the exact proposed decision; 3,506 does not guarantee the study is adequately powered. R19's 5.56-point post-hoc oracle gap is a different intervention, sample and estimand, and is not a defensible effect-size estimate for C4−P0. Twenty paired units under the same two-contrast simultaneous bound have a worst-case radius of about **66.2 percentage points**. Their outcomes cannot establish efficacy, noninferiority, equivalence or safety.

One implementation detail needs prespecification: if candidate validation has a family-dependent rejection rate, fixing a pre-validation family mixture does not necessarily fix the accepted distribution. A bounded rejection failure that aborts the run can additionally make a completed dataset conditional on acceptance. A minimal alternative below freezes already validated finite supports before any treatment outcomes, then samples from those supports. It does not establish a representative population or overcome historical/pretraining exposure uncertainty.

## Concrete candidate protocol and estimand

1. Before collection, freeze P0 and exactly one final checkpoint each for C1 and C4, a common training pool and source labels, equalized training dose and the relevant code/runtime/decoding. C1 and C4 must differ only in the intended training treatment. External teacher, human or planner corrections must be labelled as such; do not describe them as solely autonomous self-improvement.
2. Using only newly self-authored inputs and treatment-independent validity checks, form and freeze disjoint training, feasibility and confirmatory lineage supports. For this illustrative operational floor, require at least 10 distinct canonical task instances within each of the two fixed evaluation families in each of the feasibility and confirmatory supports; otherwise stop before model evaluation. This floor is a proposed feasibility rule, not an inferential power guarantee. Budget and retain every generation/validation/rejection, and stop if the fixed task-preparation cap is reached. No cap for that work is yet established, so a real study is not budget-ready.
3. Seal the confirmatory support, treatment mapping, arm ordering rule and randomization algorithm/commitment before outcome observation. Define the target distribution explicitly: first choose one of the two fixed families with probability 1/2, then draw uniformly with replacement from that family's frozen confirmatory support, with independently sampled declared environment randomness. Apply the same task and compatible environment seed to all three fixed checkpoints within a draw. Fresh independent draws of task, seed and arm order must be generated by an audited isolation mechanism; independent HMAC streams in a software fixture do not by themselves prove the actual generator/runtime is independent. Duplicate canonical draws are retained and disclosed, not renamed new families. Stop on identity drift, unapproved support access or unresolved validity/lineage conflict.
4. Primary outcome for each arm is binary terminal task success within the same frozen horizon 50. Define a normal, explicitly prespecified policy-format rejection as a completed non-success if the frozen policy stops on that output; distinguish it from an infrastructure, environment, mapping, evidence or process failure. The latter is an incomplete technical outcome: stop, preserve all work, do not silently set it to failure or substitute another draw. No recovery selector, extra environment, new model behavior or automatic retry is introduced.

The resulting claims are conditional on the particular fixed checkpoints, frozen finite support and its two-family 50/50 mixture. They do not estimate variation over new training seeds, new task families, ALFWorld as a whole, model pretraining exposure or unknown historical task exposure. If execution is provably deterministic for each fixed task/seed and the finite support itself is the intended estimand, evaluating its entire support once with the specified weights could give the exact finite-support contrast without 3,506 sampled repeats; it still would not support an unseen-population claim. That alternative needs its own preregistered design and budget, not post-hoc selection after seeing results.

## Stage rules and complete-outcome accounting

- **Feasibility:** evaluate exactly 20 planned draws (10 from each family, selected from the separately frozen feasibility support) on all three checkpoints. Cap at 60 episodes, 3,000 model generations and 3,060 reset-plus-step operations if each turn uses at most one generation and one step. A completed normal format-invalid output is an observed policy non-success if predefined; any unexplained/technical incomplete episode, wrong source or action mapping, budget breach, lost monitoring or evidence gap stops this stage. Require all 60 records complete and valid, no unresolved physical-cost tails and an independently reviewed complete budget before considering a later stage. Report pilot outcomes descriptively only; they confer no efficacy PASS. If pilot results influence the recipe, analysis, support, endpoints or sample size, freeze a new protocol and use a fresh isolated confirmatory support; do not pool the pilot into confirmation or perform unplanned repeated looks.
- **Confirmatory:** only if a separate frozen protocol, independent confirmatory support, actual implementation, complete budget and resource review are available, draw exactly 3,506 paired units from the fixed mixture above. Each yields P0/C1/C4 booleans. There is no outcome-based early stopping, unplanned sample-size increase or automatic retry. A predeclared technical/safety stop produces an incomplete study and **no fixed-N confirmatory p-value or confidence interval**; keep the recorded observations descriptive and physical costs/unknowns. The candidate Python function refuses incomplete, extra, nonbinary or duplicate/out-of-order draw indices. Caller must separately verify all unrepresented provenance/identity/isolation assumptions.
- **Complete-case analysis only:** let `D0 = success(C4)−success(P0)` and `D1 = success(C4)−success(C1)` per paired unit. Compute both net paired means and simultaneous two-sided 95% Hoeffding intervals `mean(Dj) ± sqrt(2 ln(80)/3506)`, clipped to `[-1,1]`. Report wins, losses, ties, estimated differences and both intervals. Candidate decision: if primary lower bound is **strictly greater** than +5 points, the statistical rule supports the conditional `C4−P0 > 5 points` claim; if its upper bound is nonpositive, stop the proposed positive-benefit line; otherwise label inconclusive. This does not prove no benefit, equivalence or no harm. In all cases report C4−C1; its direction is not an extra condition for the distinct C4−P0 claim. Other required provenance/technical criteria are external to this numerical rule and can veto a positive classification.
- **Separate zero-effect tests:** for each contrast, count paired wins `w` and losses `l`; ignoring ties, under the zero-difference null and IID paired draws, `w | (w+l)` follows Binomial(`w+l`, 1/2). Report the exact two-sided binomial sign/McNemar p-value (twice the smaller exact tail, capped at 1), then Holm-adjust the two prespecified p-values. Holm controls familywise error under dependence between contrasts. These tests address **zero paired difference**, not the minimum-effect hypothesis `C4−P0 ≤ 5 points`; their p-values do not validate a 5-point clinical/practical margin or the 3,506-sample power. They supplement the confidence-bound decision and cannot replace its external eligibility conditions.

## Reproducible software-only check

`paired_rule.py` implements only the fixed-N numerical rule on caller-supplied in-memory boolean fixtures; it has no asset I/O or execution side effects and does not validate IID, task construction, or actual study provenance. Eight synthetic tests passed (including 350-versus-351 net-win boundary, zero ties, exact binomial references, Holm, incomplete/nonbinary rejection and a 3,506-discordance numerical case). The statistical method and code require separate exact-byte scientific and implementation review before being attached to any formal study.
