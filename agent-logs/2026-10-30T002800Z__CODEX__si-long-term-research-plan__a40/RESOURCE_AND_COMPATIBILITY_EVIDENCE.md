## Compatibility and acceptance dependencies

The official old archive games are not drop-in equivalents to the current files: the 0.2.2 and 0.4.0 variants for common paths have different bytes and upstream changed action wording/feedback. The inspected upstream patch rewrites in place and creates backups; it may change policy-visible action ordering, maps and feedback. The existing single-game production adapter bypasses normal ALFWorld discovery checks including solvability and eligible task types. The reviewed old SI-U1 evaluator binds TRAIN, 82 tasks and three arms. Therefore archive membership and a game-path hash do not establish runtime compatibility, eligibility or semantic equivalence, and the old evaluator cannot be repurposed by swapping task paths or splitting datasets.

Long-term plan prerequisites: establish game versions, provenance and permitted data use; resolve historically exposed parents and scene/layout risks; identify an outcome-independent sampling frame with no post-result cherry-picking; separately specify and review any isolated migration and version-aware evaluation software; define a testable estimand, family-wise decision/precision, seed dependence and missing outcomes; then calculate budgets and monitor/stop rules before any scientific execution. If no adequate independent sample or budget exists, retain an inconclusive thesis result and pursue a defensible alternative instead of relabeling TRAIN as held-out.

Any subsequent reviewed run needs fresh identity and immutable evidence, fixed inputs and scope, physical-operation and token ledger including completed, failed, and externally started/incomplete actions, separately identifiable science versus engineering costs, and clear device allocation/clock accounting. Monitoring must fail closed on budget exhaustion, missing or contradictory receipts, unexpected drift, unknown ownership where the contract requires it, resource pressure, incomplete units or an unexpected process failure. Preserve raw evidence and never automatically retry without the specific prospective policy. These are planning conditions, not evidence that gates or permission already exist.
