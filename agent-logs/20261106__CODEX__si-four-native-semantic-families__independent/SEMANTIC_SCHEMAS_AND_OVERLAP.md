# Four domain-native development schemas and the unchanged overlap gate

**Result: four distinct symbolic goal/action schemas can be declared; four admissible concrete, independent lineage groups are not established.** This is static analysis of the pinned public domain and existing self-authored generator/specification metadata only. No case, PDDL problem, evaluation set or seed was generated; no parser, model, tokenizer, environment, CUDA or protected/unknown task, trajectory or schedule was accessed. `FAMILY_SCHEMAS.json` holds only abstract roles and predicates, not actual task instances.

## Domain-level definitions

For each candidate, require the final goal to be initially false, distinct useful object/receptacle identities, well-typed declared constants, a uniquely interpretable instruction, and compatibility with the **existing** public domain and grammar. Object naming, physical placement, path/solution, actual admissible commands and runtime behavior remain UNKNOWN. All action requirements below are directly derived from the pinned public `alfred.pddl`; they are conditional domain statements, not proof that a generated game is valid:

1. **Clean-and-deliver:** goal `isClean(target)` AND `inReceptacle(target,destination)`; under the stated false initial goal and pinned operators, `CleanObject` adds `isClean`, requiring a cleanable target held by the agent and agent co-located with a `SinkBasinType` receptacle. A delivery `PutObject` requires its own destination typing/containment/holding conditions. This is the existing multiscene CLEAN-DELIVER schema, not a newly established concrete group.
2. **Heat-and-deliver:** goal `isHot(target)` AND `inReceptacle(target,destination)`; `HeatObject` adds hot and removes cool, requiring held heatable target at `MicrowaveType`. All actual source and destination preconditions must independently be verified. The domain action does not in its displayed preconditions require the microwave door to open; do not assert real wrapper/rendering behavior from that.
3. **Located toggle:** goal `isToggled(target)`, initially false; `ToggleObject` requires a toggleable target in a receptacle and the agent and that receptacle at the same location. Its conditional effects toggle `isOn` and add `isToggled`; an initially on target can become off, so a natural-language instruction “turn on” is **not** equivalent to this sole goal. The intended wording must describe the chosen state/goal correctly and be independently checked. This schema's core action does not require the agent to hold the target.
4. **Tool-mediated slicing:** goal `isSliced(target)`, initially false; `SliceObject` requires a sliceable target at the agent's location and the agent holding a separately identified tool whose `objectType` is `KnifeType` or `ButterKnifeType`. The schema explicitly requires distinct target/tool and a declared path to acquire and hold the appropriate tool. Reachability, grammar surface command, required constant declarations and actual tool availability are unverified.

Given the stated goal atoms and unsatisfied initial values, each has a distinct native goal predicate and domain action label. In a **goal- and action-labelled** abstract graph preserving these constants, the four symbolic graphs are distinguishable: their goal labels and core causal preconditions are not equal under a consistent entity renaming. This narrow statement is not an admissibility, solvability, custody, non-overlap, random-sampling, or statistical independence result. CLEAN vs HEAT have particularly similar process-while-holding topology; a goal-blind comparison may regard them as the same setup. Slice and the process families may share pickup/navigation subpaths. Toggle also shares possible navigation. No arbitrary semantic color, new family identifier or separate author label defeats an existing overlap edge.

## Apply the already-reviewed rules; do not silently weaken them

There is no single authenticated, complete concrete-candidate inventory or fully specified project-wide numeric near-duplicate threshold in the reviewed material. Therefore the process below **does not freeze a replacement similarity rule** or claim to decide four independent groups:

- Reuse the existing relevant exact canonical, role-graph and action-subpath comparison for the specific generator/version; for that constructor, the independent review says recomputed common contiguous variable-length role/action subpath or matching goal-blind initial graph gives `MATCH_REVIEW_REQUIRED`. A shared action can be a broad flag: the review explicitly says it is **not automatically proof of copying or task identity**. A nonmatch remains `NO_MATCH_INCONCLUSIVE` and independence UNKNOWN. Some candidate pairs could be flagged, depending on actual graphs/traces; no actual graph or trace is claimed here.
- Preserve pre-existing nonnegotiable task/derivation/near-duplicate edges and their transitive closure across all submitted concrete candidates. The declaration checker v3 treats broad domain/grammar/family as broad nodes, but concrete parent chains, cross-partition exact identity and declared near-duplicate relations remain relevant; broad nodes must not conceal a concrete ancestor. It checks **declarations only**, with no guarantee an undeclared relationship is absent.
- For an actual matched pair, record the exact available rule ID, matching graph/subpath evidence and independent adjudication; if the frozen policy says the match is an edge, retain it and merge the entire connected components. If it says review required but does not define a conclusive threshold, do not invent either an automatic link or a separation: keep the relation UNKNOWN until the authorized reviewer resolves it under a pre-existing policy. Missing ancestry, unavailable relevant reference or inability to recompute graphs likewise yields UNKNOWN. Do not change threshold after observing outcomes to obtain four groups.
- Exact goal/action-labelled distinctness is a **separate attribute**, not a substitute for the existing goal-blind/subpath/ancestry checks. Even after all available scoped checks pass, absence of evidence of conflict is not proof of absence of protected data overlap, independently selected task generation, hidden shared parent, or model-pretraining exposure.

Consequently no number `4` can currently be attached to usable *specific lineage components*. The earlier at-most-two result was conditional on a different fixed V1 one-object/two-receptacle/two-location equivalence setting; it is not a universal theorem that four native semantic families must collapse. In the opposite direction, the four different predicate labels are not proof they remain separate under the actual frozen goal-blind and ancestral closure either.

## Practical next step without creating formal tasks

An implementer can propose, in a fresh default-disabled development generator, the four goal schemas and their full role/dependency constraints with direct source/grammar hashes, truthful structured instructions, declared operator preconditions, constants and explicit action-bound policy. Four **schema** outputs may be used for fake-only checker tests. Such work is new generator implementation and requires independent static/source review; only clean/heat multiscene generation has a prior conceptual specification, so do not claim toggle/slice generator support already exists.

Separately, before any actual case generation or model outcome, the coordinator must locate and freeze the **applicable existing** comparison/version and its ambiguity handling, initial source/exclusion inventory, and a declared concrete-parent ledger. Once an authorized DEVELOPMENT_ONLY candidate exists, independently recompute its raw graph and use the above existing checks, retaining flags and UNKNOWN and tracing connected components. If existing rules and complete admissible metadata really produce four separate components, a development study may propose them, but that finding remains subject to semantic/engine validation, custody, task permissions and a complete separately reviewed physical budget. If the rules merge them or any required relationship is unresolved, retain fewer groups / UNKNOWN and use the previously proposed single-case engineering-only question instead of changing the four-case gate or claiming self-improvement.

No output here is an executable study, a guarantee that four components will exist, or a launch clearance.
