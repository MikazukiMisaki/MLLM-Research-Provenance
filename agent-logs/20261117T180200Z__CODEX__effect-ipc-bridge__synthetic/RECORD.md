It preserves a failing synthetic test log and the later six passing tests, and retains separate synthetic parent/child journals and process receipts.

Independent review reran six tests and checked the real client/source binding, per-sequence ACK, whole child line receipt hashes and fail-stop behavior. See review/REVIEW.md and static/INTERFACE.md. This is a communication fragment, not a production-wired or review-cleared experiment: actual actor and worker, authentic native success, roster, resources, token/time budget, complete process cleanup and old-plus-new science accounting are absent. Child-reported receipt and acknowledgement are not proof of physical execution. Prior STOP/HOLD and no-retry boundaries remain in force; this artifact neither authorizes a new run nor amends any budget.
