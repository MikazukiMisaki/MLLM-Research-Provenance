## Findings

- Outcome table now explicitly maps verified success to 1, completed native failure, defined H50 exhaustion and defined parser policy failure to 0, and missing/interrupted/technical or ambiguous evidence to UNKNOWN. The v3 statement treating all “failed” cases as UNKNOWN is gone. Its primary endpoint is now correctly named **protocol-defined episode success**, not purely native terminal success. The rules remain prospective and must be implemented symmetrically without retroactive reclassification.
- Each training example's total encoded input, target and special tokens must fit 4096 and the verified trainer/model limit; generation prompt plus 16 reserved tokens must fit the separately verified effective inference context. Stated separate token-budget maxima are marginal, not permission to exceed joint context. Actual tokenizer/model/trainer/runtime capacities remain unobserved.
- The prespecified screening rule still requires all 16 training parents qualify, complete 112 evaluation outcomes, targeted−control ≥1/16, targeted−P0 ≥1/16 and ≤2 parents with strict-majority control wins. The P0 all-success/target 9/control 8 counterexample does not pass; screen is a resource-oriented exploratory criterion, not proof of significance or real improvement.
- The line “P0 is descriptive baseline” is compatible with an exploratory, nonconfirmatory analysis but may be mistaken for “P0 does not affect the decision.” For clarity, before protocol freeze state: **P0 comparison is descriptive and not a confirmatory hypothesis test; its prespecified +1/16 threshold is nevertheless a necessary follow-up resource-screen condition.** Do not change or reinterpret the threshold after outcomes.
- Same selected first episode for each of 16 preselected training parents, paired exact loss-bearing target IDs and full loss masks, seed labels 42/43/44, and 16 evaluation parent clusters remain unchanged. This is an estimate of the full state-selection policy in a selected successful corpus, not source or action causality. Three seed runs and 16 nonrandom parents cannot supply confirmatory evidence; parent-cluster bootstrap must be labeled descriptive and conditional on sampled roster and fixed training seeds.
- Conditional arithmetic remains 384 training microbatches / 96 optimizer steps, 112 evaluation episodes, 5600 maximum generation/steps, 112 resets, and 5712 steps-plus-resets. The four GPU-hour and six wall-hour values are unverified stop caps, not demonstrated capacity; real historical costs, training/device/I/O/cleanup budgets, shared ownership, and independently traceable actual materials/evaluator are still unknown.

Before any real run, freeze/independently check actual permitted source identity, data selection and trainer transformations, reproducible task/parent lineage and environment seeds, all hyperparameters, executable evaluator and resource accounting.
