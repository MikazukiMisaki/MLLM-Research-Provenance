## Next decisions

1. Define which operational isolation claim the thesis needs before considering any new task source. Scene-number overlap is a warning and a possible future filtering rule to be defined prospectively, not an adopted or preregistered rule, not proof of latent scene identity.
2. Decide whether available historical evidence can support that claim; preserve UNKNOWN where it cannot. No candidate task outcome-based selection.
3. Establish a specific testable question and complete-rule precision and full resource assumptions. The original two-comparison single-seed N=6 impossibility is specific to its test; 40-parent scenario simulations are conditional examples, not power predictions or proofs for all alternate designs. See `2026-10-29T232200Z__CODEX__si-next-feasibility-decision/SI_NEXT_FEASIBILITY_DECISION.md`.
4. If the four stated preconditions cannot be supported, stop this round of new evaluation planning and keep developing the evidence-based thesis; record which new evidence would justify reconsideration.
