# Addendum: correction to task support pool counts

The earlier `SUPPORT_EXTENSION_REVIEW.md` incorrectly called a **20 acquisition/collection + 20 feasibility + 20 confirmation** design “40+20” and treated that allocation as the existing proposal. The 60 total happens to equal the current feasibility proposal's 40+20, but the allocations and acceptance criteria are **different and not interchangeable**. This addendum corrects that statement and leaves the earlier document unchanged. It supersedes its text wherever that text identifies the 20/20/20 alternative as the existing target or requires 10 groups per family in each of three partitions as the sole existing gate.

The actual earlier `SCIENTIFIC_FEASIBILITY_PROPOSAL.md` section 1 requires **40 distinct acquisition groups (20 per family) plus 20 distinct feasibility groups (10 per family)** after validity and conservative cross-split checks. Confirmation is a **separate** future sealed pool only if a confirmatory study is sought; its number was not fixed by that requirement. If later a 20-group confirmation pool is proposed, balanced 10 per family, this produces **80 groups total**, not 60. All rows below are planning minima, not existing verified tasks, automatic proof of statistical independence, representative sampling, power, or execution authority.

| Design / pool | Acquisition total (per family) | Feasibility total (per family) | Confirmation total (per family) | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Existing feasibility proposal | 40 (20 + 20) | 20 (10 + 10) | Separate, size not fixed | 60 + any later confirmation |
| Existing proposal plus illustrative later confirmation | 40 (20 + 20) | 20 (10 + 10) | 20 (10 + 10) | 80 |
| Distinct alternative I mistakenly substituted | 20 (10 + 10) | 20 (10 + 10) | 20 (10 + 10) | 60 |

The last row is a different proposal: halving acquisition support may change repair yield and the ability to meet the originally proposed minimum four repair-bearing groups; its acquisition and downstream budget cannot be assumed equivalent. It must not silently replace the existing proposal. The default recommendation is to retain the existing 40-acquisition + 20-feasibility proposal unless the coordinator explicitly freezes a separately justified revision in advance. Before generating or evaluating cases, specify which allocation, exact valid group definition, per-family minimum, wholly disjoint transitive overlap components, custody boundary, validation cap and full resource budget apply. If confirmation is pursued, separately freeze its target, custody and budget before access, without relabeling feasibility as confirmation. Counts by nominal family must be checked after cross-family merges; a shared component cannot satisfy multiple disjoint-pool requirements.

The prior extension's illustrative conservative rule still yields at most **six candidate groups per family**, before feasibility exclusions or cross-family merges, so it satisfies neither the existing minimum of 30 per family across acquisition and feasibility, nor the alternative's 30 per family across three pools, nor 40 per family when an illustrative 20-confirmation pool is added to the existing proposal. These are upper bounds for the illustrative parametrization under that specific rule, not proof that any group is valid or independently sampled. A different justified rule or richer schema needs independent review and actual measured group counts.

Budget correction: the current feasibility proposal's model-free authoring/validation cap is up to 200 attempts with at most **five** scripted steps per attempt. The extension describes new, unimplemented conjunctive tasks that may require **eight** steps. Their eight-step calculation is a separate illustrative design, not permission to exceed the existing five-step cap or substitute its budget: revise and freeze the validation schedule and all resulting physical costs before any such witness. All other technical, physical and incomplete-operation costs remain separate and no experiments are authorized by this addendum.
