## Findings and required changes

1. **HIGH: required matched-compute comparison absent.** Implementation Phase 2 specifically asks whether the fixed recovery meaningfully differs from matched extra generation. The proposed no-recovery versus two-stage recovery comparison does not isolate the recovery procedure from additional model calls. Predefine a third paired arm at each eligible source prefix: same frozen P0, replay, state, action mapping, horizon and continuation as recovery, but substitute a fixed neutral generation for diagnosis, followed by a fixed ordinary action call. Freeze the neutral prompt and how its output is handled before outcomes. Budget two calls with the same call-level maximum output-token caps as recovery; equal caps/call count do not imply equal realized tokens, latency or semantic information, so measure each. If this control cannot be delivered, report the comparison as recovery versus normal continuation only, and Phase 2 matched-extra-generation gate UNKNOWN, not passed. Any alternative control must be preregistered and justified; do not tune it against evaluation results.

2. **HIGH: single trajectory horizon must constrain replay and continuation jointly.** The table reserves 49 replay + 1 replacement + 49 continuation steps per branch despite requiring an identical 50-step remaining-horizon comparison. The 99 is a safe loose numerical overestimate, not an undercount; it must never be read as permission to execute 99 steps. Specify r + 1 + c <= 50, r in [0,49], c <= 49-r; actual stopping earlier upon terminal state, invalid actions or failure must follow the frozen interface. Capture and compare the replayed state and valid-action map before branching and retain immutable prefix identity; each branch independently starts from the same snapshot, never a post-treatment state. Normal baseline may reuse a previously persisted source continuation only if predeclared and its full replay/state correspondence and audit are verified.

3. **HIGH: independent task groups and custody not established.** A hash or different RNG seed is not evidence of independent task origin or prior nonexposure. Freeze generator/version, allowed source lineage, concrete-ancestor/near-duplicate graph, and grouping algorithm before output/outcome review. Common abstract domain/template is not automatically the same as a concrete derivative, but all concrete derivatives/declared related instances must stay in one partition; do not redefine edges after seeing effects. For 20-unit inference verify 20 eligible independent groups if the design requires group independence; otherwise report components and fixed-set results only. An independent custodian and unobserved assessment contents require operational evidence, not a self-assertion. If custody, admissible provenance or sufficient support cannot be obtained, keep a development-only pilot with limits explicitly recorded. The earlier 40 acquisition + 20 feasibility support proposal and this 12-episode pilot are distinct designs; 12 executed acquisition episodes are not evidence that 40 independent eligible acquisition groups exist. If retaining prior 40+20 support, separately verify group availability and selected subset before any use; no claim of equivalence without an explicit amendment.

4. **HIGH: deterministic scientific selection and failure treatment are unspecified.** Before looking at recovery outcomes, freeze eligible source prefix criteria, ordering, how 12 acquisition episodes and at most four candidate source groups are chosen, per-group maximums, duplicate screening, and required M/K counts. Use observables available at the branch point, or clearly label a retrospectively defined target population. Do not choose successful recovery attempts as a substitute for representative selection. Label training data only from verified, provenance-bound action/continuation outcomes; a failed, invalid, unknown or unverified correction is never an accepted target. The current 'four examples from multiple sources' does not establish four independent corrected source groups; if the earlier protocol requires four groups, retain that requirement or explicitly register a smaller study and its different claim. Scientific failure at the prescribed horizon is an observed outcome; a technical interruption, missing return, failed replay or unresolved side effect is UNKNOWN, not task failure or success. Preserve its start/partial evidence, stop further execution on a technical failure, and do not retry, silently discard, backfill or form a completed-pair estimate for a missing arm. Describe the resulting partial study without outcome-dependent imputation; prespecify any future missingness sensitivity analysis.

5. **MEDIUM: define exact two-level estimand and loss.** For task/group i and paired training seed s, record d_is = Y_C4,is - Y_C1,is in {-1,0,1}; report each seed's average across observed complete prespecified units, each task's three paired effects, wins/losses/ties and infrastructure UNKNOWN separately. The descriptive aggregate is (1/3) sum_s (1/n) sum_i d_is only if all prespecified cells complete, with P0 comparison stated separately. Three seed-pairs are three training realizations evaluated on the same tasks, not 60 independent tasks. Training-seed variation, evaluation-task variation and execution randomness are distinct; inference to task or training-seed populations requires a predeclared sampling model and enough independent task components/seed realizations. Do not compute a task-level CI by treating seed-by-task cells as independent. Fixed same seed/config/order does not itself prove identically realized dropout/random streams across diverging training trajectories. Report that comparison C1 versus C4 isolates the assigned weighting policy with its resulting gradient-scale, clipping and optimizer dynamics, not pure weight effects; C1 versus untrained P0 also changes training compute.

For each optimizer update window W, use single-example forwards, accumulate numerator sum_(j in W) w_j sum_(t in active(j)) loss_jt, then divide by sum_(j in W) |active(j)|, including partial final windows. In C1 all w=1; in C4 use full-dataset alpha=M/(M+3K), weights 4alpha for K and alpha otherwise. M,K count distinct examples, not tokens. This example-level formula does not ensure equal weighted token contribution or gradient norms when examples have different active-token lengths. The proposed window size four is a new design choice; explicitly record the change from earlier eight-example-window semantics and test exact gradient accumulation, partial windows, masks and clipping. No claims of unchanged prior protocol.

6. **MEDIUM: arithmetic is correct within the proposal's stated loose assumptions, but not a complete physical budget.** 12*50=600; 4*(49+1+49)=396; 20*7*50=7000; 156 resets + 7996 steps = 8152 starts. Generations 600+4*(2+49)+7000=7804; 7804*4096=31,965,184 input-token cap and 7800*32+4*64=249,856 output-token cap. Training 32*6=192 example forward/backward starts, ceil(32/4)*6=48 update starts, and at most 196,608 padded positions if all examples padded to 1024; active-token compute is separately measured. These maxima presume per-call token limits checked before dispatch and no extra calls, including error paths; 14 model loads is an assumption requiring verified implementation, not an independently established upper bound.

Illustrative tightened accounting *if* the three-arm control in finding 1 is implemented exactly as specified and the normal continuation reuses the authenticated original source without extra run: four recovery and four matched-control branches each cost at most one reset, 50 steps, and 51 generation calls (two intervention/control calls + at most 49 continuation calls). With 12 source episodes and 140 evaluations, this yields at most 160 resets, 8000 physical steps, 8160 reset+step starts, 8008 generation starts, 32,800,768 input tokens, and 256,512 output tokens, assuming each recovery/control first call <=64 and each action <=32. These are conservative independent upper bounds; eligibility and shared horizon may lower attainable values. If retaining the original loose 99-step reservation, the corresponding reset+step allowance is 8356; do not silently replace an authorized ledger or lower any existing cap. If normal continuation needs a newly executed third branch, add its separate reset, steps, generation and time. Count every started external action, including failed and unresolved calls. These figures exclude model loads, CPU tokenization, environment construction/close, task/compiler/translator operations and descendants, training compute, idle/cleanup, real GPU time/memory, disk, wall time and prior starts; all require separately estimated/measured limits and honest UNKNOWN. A complete physical budget requires a real training fit assessment and independently reviewed resource controls. Do not infer training fit from successful inference.

7. **MEDIUM: statistical precision claims are illustrative, not power or proof.** For iid bounded differences in [-1,1], Hoeffding's two-sided 95% half-width is sqrt(2 ln(40)/n): n=20 gives 0.60736, and n=738 gives <=0.10. The variance-based normal calculation ceil(1.96^2*0.20/0.10^2)=77 is only a rough precision estimate under assumed discordance 0.20, small mean difference, suitable independence and large-sample approximation; it is neither an assured sample size nor a power calculation. Define population and margin, two-sided confidence or power, correlation/cluster unit and multiplicity in advance if confirmatory inference is ever claimed. Fixed constructed cases with unknown distribution warrant descriptive effects only. No planned 20-case development run can establish a modest improvement with narrow uncertainty.
