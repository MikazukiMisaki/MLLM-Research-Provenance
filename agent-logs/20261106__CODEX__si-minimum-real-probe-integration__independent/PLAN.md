It is a feasibility check of a frozen P0 agent on one self-authored symbolic task. It is not training, a success-rate estimate, a held-out evaluation, or evidence for self-improvement. The user has already authorized appropriate project work; the outstanding conditions below are technical and scientific, not a request for another general approval.

## Minimal real connection to implement

1. **Fixed, independently reviewed launcher and claim.** A separate controller launches only the exact reviewed worker with a fixed absolute interpreter, argv, isolated environment and own output directory; it checks component/source hashes immediately before and after, records source/task/manual/grammar/domain identities, snapshot location, device binding and argv/environment digest, and creates the single-use claim before spawning. No broad project-data searches. Bind local model snapshot identity, tokenizer and PEFT adapter, library versions and source. The earlier five model-shard digest observations with overall exit 1 and current metadata match are *historical/metadata-only*, not a fresh hash or proof of bytes loaded. If fresh byte verification is not performed, record `MODEL_BYTES_FRESHLY_VERIFIED=false`; do not claim exact-byte reproduction. Preserve any observed partial load cost and no retry.
2. **Shared-device admission without ownership claims.** The real launcher invokes the exact frozen sampler in separate self-owned subprocesses. Obtain two successful device samples separated by at least 5 seconds immediately before launch and verify both contain the same expected A40 UUID/name and reported free memory ≥24,576 MiB; both host views must report MemAvailable ≥32 GiB and output filesystem free ≥2 GiB. These are conservative *proposed engineering floors*, not empirically measured model fit, reservation or a guarantee; revise only through documented review if actual evidence supports it. Record utilization but do not require GPU idleness or exclusivity. Host MemAvailable may not reflect the worker's effective cgroup limit. If accessible finite cgroup/ancestor limits are demonstrably applicable and insufficient, stop; if mapping or ancestors are unknown, record UNKNOWN and do not present the host number as guaranteed worker memory. The last saved device snapshot (2026-11-06 16:24:47 UTC filesystem mtime, not trusted time) was 10,710 MiB free, below the proposed floor; a future launch must take fresh samples, not reuse it. Nominal 8-billion BF16 weight bytes alone are roughly 15,259 MiB before activations and loading overhead, not a measured placement.
3. **Fixed device and local model path.** Set `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES` to the exact target GPU UUID in the worker and use logical `cuda:0`; normalize a UUID only for comparison. Record exact CUDA/PyTorch device UUID and device name after initialization and before model load; stop on missing/mismatch. Use pinned local model/adapter/tokenizer and explicit offline options. After loading, record actual parameter/buffer devices, effective generation configuration and any observable device map; fail on unexpected placement/offload rather than changing dtype, quantization, prompt or scientific settings. Parameter device/map does not measure every native allocation or prove model fit.
4. **Finite observed sampling, not continuous certainty.** Run the sampler in its own fixed, bounded subprocess before worker start and at a target interval of 10 seconds during load/generation/step; the owner records its own monotonic and qualified UTC start/end timestamps around every call and preserves each raw snapshot/hash/rc. An independently enforced sample deadline (proposed 8 seconds) is needed because the sampler's internal limits do not bound process creation, hashing, fsync or uninterruptible kernel work. A normal interval with no observation is explicitly `UNKNOWN_BETWEEN_SAMPLES`; never infer a peak, other-user identity or absence of allocations. If a required sample is missing/malformed, wrong UUID/name, times out, cannot be reaped, or the observation gap exceeds 20 seconds, issue fail-stop for the own worker and mark the interval/effects UNKNOWN. Proposed runtime device-wide free floor: stop own worker on a valid observation below 4,096 MiB; device-wide usage is not attributable to it. Thresholds are proposed risk controls, not guarantees against rapid concurrent allocation or OOM. A SIGSTOP or uninterruptible owner may prevent timely response; record unknown rather than assert enforcement.
   For the known direct worker only, the owner should also sample raw `/proc/<own-child>/status` `VmRSS` with its retained pidfd/identity binding; propose stopping that worker if observed RSS exceeds 40 GiB or its required own-process reading is missing beyond the same 20-second limit. Do not inspect other processes. RSS is resident memory of that direct process, not virtual memory, an instantaneous hard limit, or total descendant/cgroup memory; peaks between reads remain UNKNOWN. This observation and enforcement are **not implemented** in the frozen sampler and require tests/review.
5. **No signaling unrelated processes.** The owner creates and retains a pidfd for its exact direct worker and a separate pidfd for each own sampler; on a stop, signal only the corresponding known direct process through its pidfd, wait with bounded grace, and if necessary escalate through that same pidfd. Do not use numeric-PID `killpg`, infer ownership from `nvidia-smi`, or terminate another user's workload. The current fake supervisor uses an insufficient `killpg(child.pid, ...)` helper and **must not be reused as a real cleanup implementation**. A controlled worker should set parent-death signal before model/world work and immediately check its expected parent; note this is per process, is cleared across fork in a new child, and has parent-thread, credential/exec and registration-race caveats. Set owner subreaper if available and wait for known adopted descendants without claiming it prevents escape. Parent death after a liveness check, watchdog death, lost pipe copies, uninterruptible work and descendants changing groups/sessions can defeat cleanup. Preserve UNKNOWN and never signal an unidentified process. If pidfd creation or required parent/liveness binding fails before work, do not start the worker.
6. **Honest worker and physical ledger.** A durable append-only log records started/returned/failed/unknown around environment construct/load/reset/step/close, CUDA init, tokenizer/base/adapter/model load and generation; owner records observed child status, stop, timeout, periodic sample hashes and external final file/head hashes. Preserve raw initial feedback, exact rendered prompt/input token IDs, raw generation/output IDs, parser result/action map, selected public command and post-step result; hidden grounding/facts stay separate from prompt and are used only for audit. Store resource use and unattributable GPU/host readings with scope. Events left open or lost do not become zero; reserve declared caps and mark actual/unobserved overage UNKNOWN. Process stdout/stderr, immutable run identity, output paths, source/model/input metadata and before/after hashes remain available for local audit. A local claim/event chain is not by itself an independent trusted anchor; save a separately owned receipt and later upload safe, hash-verifiable evidence. Keep raw machine-specific or restricted material local unless reviewed.

## Outcomes and stop rules

- `COMPLETED_ONE_STEP`: actual direct worker exited 0, validated public task/one generation/unique grounded command/one step and required records, independent review finds no contradictory failure or unobservable required operation. This is an engineering observation only.
- `COMPLETED_INVALID_SELECTION`: actual one generation returned but parser produced an invalid/non-grounded selection, so no step was taken; preserve raw output and classify as a failed/limited engineering behavior, not a successful action. Do not retry or substitute a scripted command.
- `STOP_FAILED_OR_UNKNOWN`: any incorrect goal, ambiguous grounding, input drift, device/placement mismatch, budget breach, missing required record, monitor failure, resource stop, launch exception or timeout. Preserve actual exit code and all partial ledgers, explicit started/completed/failed counts and unknowns. Never mark a failed operation as completed, and never report an unobserved operation as physically absent. If direct child or descendants cannot be proven gone, report that uncertainty and stop; no signal may be sent to an unverified process.

Success must require all mandatory result and monitoring checks even if a child exits 0 early. Earlier fake watchdog versions demonstrated that exit-first ordering can falsely return success. Sampling cannot prove no violation occurred between observations; every outcome carries this limitation. An external, independently reviewed semantic audit must reconstruct prompt/token selection, mapping and step from raw events before a one-step claim; neither child rc0 nor a hash chain alone suffices.

## Decision when cgroup ancestors cannot be verified

For this **one bounded engineering probe**, verified ancestor limits are not an artificial unconditional prerequisite: allow a decision using fresh device-wide free-memory observations, host MemAvailable, own-direct-worker RSS, storage floor, the single-call/15-minute caps and a functioning stop path, while recording `CGROUP_EFFECTIVE_HEADROOM=UNKNOWN`, any visible literal and its mapping uncertainty. Stop on an observed applicable finite shortage, missing mandatory host/GPU/own-process measurements, excess observed RSS, or controller/monitor failure. This is a transparent, residual resource risk: an unseen ancestor or other cgroup member can cause OOM before any sampled floor fires, and no minimum cgroup capacity or hard instantaneous RSS bound is claimed. Sharing is allowed; never kill other processes. If this residual uncertainty cannot be tolerated in the actual shared context, stop rather than reclassify UNKNOWN as sufficient capacity.

This narrowly scoped exception **does not automatically carry to a formal multi-run training/evaluation campaign**. That campaign requires a separately established effective resource/containment plan or equivalent enforceable allocation, complete cumulative budget and independent data/statistical design, with unresolved scope explicitly addressed before its own review. A one-step engineering observation cannot establish those requirements.
