## Current references [1]–[9]

1. **[ALFWorld, Shridhar et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.03768v2)** introduces the linked text/embodied framework. The manuscript's text-only use is accurate; its 82 TRAIN tasks must not be portrayed as the original benchmark's held-out embodied evaluation.
2. **[Qwen3 Technical Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.09388)** supports the family and availability of thinking/non-thinking modes. It does not independently identify the exact P0 adapter, seed, original revision or past task exposure of this research run; those are local-run evidence, correctly qualified in the draft.
3. **[DAgger, Ross et al., AISTATS 2011](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v15/ross11a.html)** motivates dependence of observations on prior policy actions and covers expert labels on learner-visited states. It does not demonstrate that SI-U1's failures were caused by distribution shift, nor is the C self-success data equivalent to DAgger.
4. **[Micheli & Fleuret, EMNLP 2021](https://aclanthology.org/2021.emnlp-main.734/)** §§2.2–2.3 is the closest directly identified antecedent: expert demonstration initialization, then iterative action-token optimization on the agent's successful ALFWorld trajectories. The existing draft accurately cites it. It already rules out claiming first use of action-only self-success training after expert initialization in ALFWorld. Their iterative updates and evaluation are not our one-shot, common-P0 B/C supervised-dose-matched comparison; the cited sections do not establish or exclude every other similar control in the literature. Results were reported across five seeds, whereas SI-U1 has one training seed per continuation.
5. **[Re-ReST, Dou et al., EMNLP 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.861/)** studies self-generated thought/action data and a reflection model that uses external feedback to improve lower-quality trajectories; ALFWorld is one task. The draft's description is supported. The reflection pipeline and evaluation differ from SI-U1's action-only selected trajectories; its reported gain is not a matched measurement of SI-U1.
6. **[Sarukkai et al., NeurIPS 2025](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/5d1f02132ef51602adf07000ca5b6138-Abstract-Conference.html)** mainly builds and curates an in-context database. The official [conference paper](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/5d1f02132ef51602adf07000ca5b6138-Paper-Conference.pdf) §6.4 and App. F additionally fine-tunes GPT-4o-mini on self-collected examples, including ALFWorld, supporting the current reference's both-in-context-and-fine-tuning claim. It begins with a small human-provided example set. Do not describe it as merely prompting or as an independent comparison against SI-U1. **Bibliographic update:** the current draft cites the valid arXiv v3 but omits its now-verified NeurIPS 2025 main-conference publication; use the official venue record in a versioned bibliography revision, retaining a preprint link only if useful.
7. **[Lauffer et al., arXiv:2512.14895v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.14895v1)** compares expert, fully on-policy and expert-corrected rollout data in software-engineering tasks and uses common rejection/repetition filtering. This supports the draft's qualified comparison. In the 7B figure each model also includes 1,000 expert trajectories and 32B corrections are mixed with expert data, so it is not a pure demonstration of isolated data-source causality; it is not an ALFWorld result. The inspected v1 was cited as arXiv; no final proceedings status was established here.
8. **[Zhang et al., ICML 2026, PMLR 306](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/zhang26m.html)** study agent experience-derived world modeling and reflective supervision mixed with expert demonstrations, including ALFWorld. In the final conference PDF p.6 they claim the same step budget per environment; Appendix B p.16 qualifies this as matched total updates for implicit world modeling and matched epochs for self-reflection. The current draft makes exactly that distinction and does not equate collection cost. This is not a pure self-success-vs-expert action-only contrast. **Bibliographic update:** the current arXiv v3 is real but the official ICML 2026 published venue, volume 306, pp.154443–154475 should be cited in the separately revised bibliography.
9. **[Agarwal et al., NeurIPS 2021](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/hash/f514cec81cb148559cf475e7426eed5e-Abstract.html)** supports caution that few independent runs make benchmark point estimates unreliable. It does not validate random sampling of our fixed TRAIN schedule or estimate between-seed variation from one seed.

The existing introduction accurately calls the common-P0 result a *nonrandomized paired comparison of two specified pipelines*, describes different training tasks and states as confounding, and avoids asserting an efficacy, equivalence, non-harm or new-algorithm result. The [6] and [8] entries are valid preprint citations rather than fabricated papers; the finding concerns identifying the final venue and edition, not a reason to invalidate the empirical result.

## Directly relevant omitted primary work

- **[ReAct Meets ActRe: A³T, COLM 2024](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=0VLBwQGWpA)** uses generated rationales and successful/failed trajectories for contrastive self-training in ALFWorld and WebShop. It demonstrates that agent-generated successes, failures and multi-round improvement are established lines of work. Its ReAct rationales, contrastive policy-gradient objective and multiple rounds are not SI-U1's one-shot action-only supervised continuation. Direct fetch 403 is recorded; the review browser provided the official conference PDF and abstract.
- **[Reflexion, Shinn et al., NeurIPS 2023](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/1b44b878bb782e6954cd888628510e90-Abstract-Conference.html)** incorporates feedback-derived verbal reflections into memory for later trials, including ALFWorld. Its cross-trial parameter-free improvement is not SI-U1's adapter update or R19's same-prefix fixed-duration recovery. This establishes that agent self-improvement need not mean a model weight update and provides recovery/reflection context.
- **[Grounding First, Ballal and V R, REALM 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.realm-1.52/)**, conference proceedings dated 29 October 2026, audits ALFWorld admissible-action execution, recovery triggers, composite treatment and success construction. It reports implementation-reported success labels with important runtime limitations and notes confounding from counter resets and termination changes. This closely precedes the thesis's submission and is a useful explicit comparator for R19's treatment/interface audit. It uses a Reflexion-style multi-trial wrapper, not R19's measured common K1–K5 path or its recorded CONTINUE suffix; do not treat its results or labels as directly exchangeable with R19. The basic idea of auditing ALFWorld action, parser, recovery and outcome contracts is not uniquely new to this thesis.
- **[DARC, Wang et al., arXiv:2608.11772v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.11772v1)** is an August 2026 preprint on diagnosis-guided selective interventions and success/cost-aware policy choice, including ALFWorld. Its own analysis states resettable tasks, verifier-based retries and ordered short-circuit cost assumptions. It motivates placing intervention choice and budget in context; its post-failure retry policy is not R19's within-episode K-step handoff, and it should not be imported as proof of an online R19 selector or fair causal isolation.
- **[AgentDebug, Zhu et al., arXiv:2509.25370v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25370v1)** introduces agent-error taxonomy, annotated failure trajectories including ALFWorld, and targeted feedback for recovery. It is pertinent to positioning the project's failure audit; the paper's model-aided classification and interventions differ from our retrospective descriptive sample, which does not independently identify causal roots.
- **[Self-imitation Learning for Action Generation in Text-based Games, Shi et al., EACL 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.eacl-main.50/)** adapts a model using valuable prior trajectories and confidence-based candidate pruning for an RL agent. It is related text-game self-imitation, not evidence of the SI-U1 same-P0 source contrast or of use of ALFWorld specifically.
- **[Last Step Matters, Li et al., EMNLP 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.475/)** assesses early uncertainty for failure prediction in deep-research agents, and reports poor early discrimination in its own setting. It is relevant context for any claim that online stopping can be inferred from early signals, but its setting and signal are different; neither it nor R19 proves early stopping impossible across tasks.

Prioritized manuscript work: cite the closest missing action-interface/recovery paper and Reflexion around R19, discuss A³T and text-game self-imitation around self-training, and consider DARC/AgentDebug and uncertainty-based stopping as contrasted contemporary literature. These are citation and interpretation tasks, not recommendations to import their architectures or run them.

## Defensible contribution wording

> This thesis documents a nonrandomized paired comparison of two specified continuation-data pipelines from a common expert-initialized policy on a fixed ALFWorld TRAIN-development schedule. It matches family-level parent counts and supervised-token dose, reports the inconclusive outcomes and physical research costs, and examines selected failures. A separate bounded-recovery study analyzes measured fixed handoff durations and a hindsight-only opportunity envelope with explicit treatment, replay, selection and cost limitations. These results do not isolate data source as a single causal factor, establish generalization, or validate an adaptive stopping policy.

The design's exact combination may be useful within the thesis, but this targeted search cannot establish that no prior publication used a similar combination or that matching these controls is unique. A qualified empirical and auditing contribution is supported; claiming a novel self-training algorithm, success improvement, first success-conditioned self-imitation, first ALFWorld recovery or a real online oracle is not. Actual SI-U1 counts and their caveats remain those of the separately accepted result; R19 remains an independent exploratory study, with 32/36 as a hindsight any-K envelope and 30/36 fixed K and CONTINUE baselines.

No local scientific code, figure, source task, paper draft or provenance index was altered. No model, CUDA, ALFWorld, scientific evaluator or new experiment was run. Final reference formatting, permissions, further source coverage and supervisor review remain open.
