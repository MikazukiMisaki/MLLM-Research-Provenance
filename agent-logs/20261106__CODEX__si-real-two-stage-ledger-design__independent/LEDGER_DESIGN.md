## Identity, persistence and count semantics

Create a unique attempt directory and immutable no-retry claim before external work, binding the exact case, code/entrypoint, interpreter, env config, prompts, adapter/base source, owner and allowed operation caps. Open owner journal and raw-evidence store with exclusive creation, durable directory entries, monotonic sequence and case/attempt/op IDs. Use an append-only framed record and hash chain, directory/file fsync, exact-source manifests, externally captured owner/worker exit and a final reconciliation record; an incomplete tail, failed fsync, failed ACK, parent death or unreadable bytes is UNKNOWN, not empty. Store raw outputs in a bounded separate durable file, hash them, then commit their reference and fsync before any downstream parser/action step; a raw write/encoding failure is UNKNOWN and stops scientific operations. Failure evidence can itself fail to persist, so absence of a failure marker never proves no side effect.

For every operation with possible external/physical effects, before invoking it worker sends `OP_PRECALL{op_id, kind, inputs_hash, index, budget_reserved}`, owner checks order and cap, persists/fsyncs and ACKs, then worker may enter the call. PRECALL is a **conservative consumed-start/reservation**, not proof CPU/GPU actually executed after ACK; a crash between ACK and call is indistinguishable. After actual return, persist/ACK `OP_RETURN{op_id, receipt}` or on a caught exception best-effort `OP_FAILED{op_id, exception_class, partial_receipt}`, and never retry. START/reservation without trustworthy return/failure is `OPEN_UNKNOWN`; a failure can still include completed/partial external effects. Duplicate IDs, return-before-start, mismatched kind/order, missing raw evidence and contradictory terminal records fail closed. Capture both op start/return and attempt outcome; do not infer whole-program rc from a child's receipt. Cache hits, initialization and releases have their own truthful events rather than a new physical-load claim.

Single-shot claim is scoped to this fixed path and reviewed process; file copying/same-user mutation and a power failure across separate files are not globally tamper-proof. Final record states such limitations. A hash chain proves recorded sequence/bytes relative to its anchored head, not independence, task privacy, truthful hardware activity or lack of descendants. Resolve a persisted claim with missing/incomplete journal as consumed/UNKNOWN, not permission for another run.
