## Sample-count semantics and observations

There is a distinct, reproducible **label/acceptance semantics** issue. `watchdog.py:213,222–232` polls the worker, then calls a blocking sampler and unconditionally increments `validated_running_samples` after the sampler returns valid bytes. A separate pure-double review test modeled a worker exiting inside that sampler, before the measurement is returned: the fake watchdog returned 0 and recorded one `validated_running_samples`, despite the fake worker being exited at sampling return. The recorded event order was admission raw/valid, worker started, runtime sample raw/valid, cleanup; escaped descendants were UNKNOWN. This demonstrates that the field is not proof the worker was alive at sample time. It does not show an actual GPU event or a demonstrated safety violation in a real run, and it does not invalidate the targeted EOF/deadline tests.

The supported interpretation is a valid sample **started after a poll that saw the fake child as running**, with its relationship to actual sample time and process exit unknown. Rename/document that distinction and define what the study needs as its acceptance condition. Checking a child again after the sample can conservatively identify some ambiguous intervals, but separate polling cannot provide atomic, gap-free monitoring; no such unrealistic requirement is implied here. A model may exit during any monitoring gap, and a valid device-wide sample does not prove attribution to it.

The real entrypoint is still hard-disabled (`:15–22,257–265`); there is no real sampling, host-memory supervision, trusted process identity or output binding. Cleanup reports escaped descendants UNKNOWN (`:80–106,240–248`) even when the leader was reaped. Identity checks followed by a process-group signal are non-atomic, and an unidentifiable, dead, blocked or orphaned process must remain unknown; do not signal unrelated processes. Watchdog death, leaked control writers, Popen or fsync stalls and OS scheduling can defeat strict deadlines; sampling/termination latency and in-flight costs need a separate enforceable policy. The recorded fake `rc=0` is not a guarantee of clean descendants, scientific success, sufficient free memory or genuine model fit. No real GPU telemetry, CUDA, model, environment, historical task or trajectory was accessed in this review.
