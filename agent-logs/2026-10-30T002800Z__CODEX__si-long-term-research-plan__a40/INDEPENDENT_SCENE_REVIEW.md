# Independent recount of historical scene-number proxies

Status: INDEPENDENT_METADATA_RECOUNT_MATCH; SCENE_SEPARATION_NOT_ESTABLISHED. This is a research planning audit, not evaluation, causal evidence, or launch authority. Executed `python recount.py` from this directory. The script reconstructs candidate membership from the official ZIP member paths, metadata exclusions, protected historical parent and identity sets, and streaming SHA256 checks. It does not extract archives or interpret candidate game/trajectory content. The prior script was inspected only to identify source files and input fields; the independent reconstruction and aggregation were implemented separately. Input file hashes, counts, and source limitations are recorded in `INDEPENDENT_SCENE_RECOUNT.json`.

## Reconstruction and results

Under the previously documented conservative policy (including the protected earlier proposal), old-only ZIP members contribute 950 paths / 345 distinct parent names. Existing local candidates contribute 17 paths / 6 names. The combined set contains 350 distinct parent names; its valid_unseen subset contains 24 distinct parent names. For the combined set, 346 / 350 parent-name final numeric suffixes occur in the union of four previously documented categories: source identities in the balanced expert dataset, B/C materialization task-exposure identities, the SI-U1 schedule, and actual G1 trajectory identities. The respective per-category counts are 322, 210, 151 and 24, and overlap between categories means they must not be summed. For valid_unseen, all 24 / 24 suffixes occur in the actual G1 record, while no suffix occurs in the other three enumerated categories. This is **label overlap**, not proof the candidate task or scene has been evaluated.

The suffix operation rejects a parent name not matching a final hyphen followed by decimal digits and converts that final number to an integer. As a supporting sample (not a general theorem), for 1,410 prior SFT candidate-source records the corresponding pre-existing source `traj_data.json` reports a `scene.scene_num` equal to the suffix and a `scene.floor_plan` equal to `FloorPlan` plus that number. Those are historical SFT **candidate pool** source records: this check does not establish that each was used in training. The current candidate tasks' trajectory bodies and game contents were not decoded. Labels with the same integer were not demonstrated to have equal geometry, task dynamics, object states or policy exposure, especially across dataset versions. Conversely, a different label is not proof of scene independence or no historical exposure.

## Actual evidence versus inputs that were only planned

- G1 is directly verified using its 60-row raw trajectories, separately hash-bound to `run_manifest.json` along with the frozen schedule. For every unique index, raw task ID, split and source-game SHA match the schedule; all 60 are valid_unseen, the manifest accepted count is 60, and the consumed registry identity set is equal. The actual 60 rows have 41 distinct parent names. The audit only uses the recorded identity and metadata fields; it does not use G1 results to pick candidates. Unrecorded activity cannot be ruled out.
- SI-U1 schedule identity is part of an independently accepted completed fixed-schedule evaluation (three arms × 82 episodes); the acceptance says all 246 records were reconstructed against the schedule. The schedule is not counted as merely an unrun proposal, but the label overlap is not an individual candidate execution. This recount does not repeat that entire independent journal audit.
- B/C materialization parent names are read from the two materialization manifests. The previously accepted real training results and authentic supervisor exits independently bind their manifest hashes and completed status. This identifies training-source provenance; it does not imply later candidate games with matching labels were presented to the model or played.
- Balanced expert dataset IDs identify materialized source rows; their presence establishes data-source identity, not proof each recorded source was executed by the target policy or each matching candidate was trained on. Separate SFT candidate pools and the earlier proposal are used for conservative filtering; membership in either is not evidence of real execution.

## Interpretation and action

Under a prospective requirement of disjoint scene-number labels from these historical categories, only 4 / 350 candidate parent names survive the enumerated overlap screen, and no valid_unseen parent survives its match to actual G1 labels. This does **not** establish that the four are independent or executable; it is a conditional metadata screen under this enumerated history, not an availability count, generalization finding, estimate of leak frequency, or universal impossibility claim. Do not weaken the disjointness requirement after seeing outcomes, and do not equate actual G1's parent or task identity with all other tasks on the same numbered floorplan. Whether such label-level disjointness is required must be chosen prospectively for the intended scientific claim; unresolved source-version compatibility, historical records, model pretraining exposure, available sample size, estimand/precision, and full budget remain independent gates.
