# Task generator: independent review, shareable summary

Status: **one development family only; no independent test support established**. Static source review found one self-authored containment goal template with 12 combinations of initial openness and distractor count. These combinations are not two demonstrated task-goal families. Their declared 6/6 split is a design placeholder, and under a strict single-factor-neighbor relation the variants are connected; they do not establish separation by lineage. Previously completed direct-engine work supports only the explicitly recorded small set of development witnesses, not the validity of all 12 configurations or a second family.

To build the proposed within-family pilot, first define and implement a second goal semantic using the same public environment; independently check the task statement against the actual goal and feasible actions on development examples. Freeze the generator, canonicalization, concrete derivation/near-duplicate rules, accepted distribution and rejection limits; develop disjoint supports without treating shared domain/family membership itself as leakage. Establish a common acquisition pool, traceable corrections and genuine C1/C4 checkpoints, enforce the full resource budget, and independently review execution. If the proposed feasibility and confirmation screening floors of ten distinct cases in each of two families are retained, they require at least 40 validated cases across those two supports, plus separate development/acquisition support; this is not a sample-size or power guarantee.

Twenty three-arm horizon-50 paired draws have a conditional evaluation ceiling of 3,000 generations and 3,060 reset-plus-step calls when each decision generates at most once. It excludes task generation, validation, training, failed operations and other costs. Shared-account data custody and unknown historical/pretraining exposure must not be represented as proven independent holdout. A technical or feasibility success is not evidence of a treatment effect.
