# Independent 0.4.2 changed-game candidate metadata recount

Status: INDEPENDENT_RECOUNT_MATCH; NOT_A_NEW_TASK_POOL_OR_EVALUATION. `recount_042.py` independently reconstructs protected identities, protected parents, proposal protection, known original game and trajectory SHA sets from the source records used in the earlier independent scene audit. It streams every member of all three downloaded 0.2.2/0.4.0/0.4.2 ZIPs, adds game hashes belonging to protected identities or parents in all three versions, and hashes local trajectory bytes without parsing candidate bodies. The source files and ZIPs were hashed before and after the scan. G1 actual raw trajectories were compared against the manifest-bound schedule by index, task, split and original game SHA: 60/60 match, with no use of task outcomes for filtering.

The 0.4.2 asset has 334 game paths whose bytes differ from the 0.4.0 asset at the same paths. Applying the documented existing proposal, known-use identity/parent, accepted-family, protected game/trajectory byte and local trajectory existence filters leaves 17 paths / six parents, all in `valid_unseen`. The other 317 are removed by protected identity or parent, before subsequent filters. The six remaining parent names have two distinct numeric suffixes; both suffixes occur among G1's actually recorded 60 identities, so all six parents have that proxy overlap. Numeric suffixes are not proof of matching scene geometry or individual exposure; those six parents are not authenticated held-out units by this audit.

**Deduplication finding:** all 17 remaining task identities equal the 17 identities in the previous independently reconstructed conservative *local* candidate set; no additional task identity is created by the 0.4.2 game bytes. The 17 are not to be added to the previously reported six local parents. Version bytes are distinct artifacts for the same path; this does not establish behavioral difference or suitability of either version. Candidate game/trajectory bodies and results were not parsed or emitted, and no model, CUDA or environment ran. The existing audit is conditional on named protected sources, available files, and the prior proposal; missing historical records, identity collisions, content semantics, solvability, license and version compatibility remain unresolved.
