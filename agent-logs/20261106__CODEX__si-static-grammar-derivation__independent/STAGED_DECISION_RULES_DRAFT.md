# Staged decision rules for the next SI study

Status: proposed, not yet frozen or executable. This note complements the three-arm addendum and requires independent review of the actual task generator, training, runtime and complete cost before any efficacy run.

1. **Engineering interface.** Verify exact own-task input and resource bindings, actual reset/goal extraction, then at most the separately reviewed and bounded P0 one-action model probe. A parser failure or unusable action is an engineering finding; preserve it and stop according to the one-shot contract. It is not a success-rate sample. A shared GPU may be used when the reviewed capacity and monitoring conditions are met; sharing does not imply exclusivity, and unknown process ownership is not attributed.
2. **Acquisition and training feasibility.** Independently review a training-only acquisition plan, budget and lineage isolation before collection. Record every attempted acquisition, failure, correction source and resulting sample. Require an actual common pool with nonzero target and nontarget counts under a prespecified label rule, a verified training/evaluation support boundary, and matched encoding, presentations, optimizer conditions and final-checkpoint policy. Demonstrate the production weighted loss on real-runtime-compatible fixtures, then independently review exact code and costs. If all criteria cannot be met, stop this proposed C1/C4 comparison rather than fabricate or reuse unknown labels. A correction supplied by an external teacher, planner or human must be named as such and changes the interpretation of self-improvement.
3. **Small feasibility stage.** Only after separate exact implementation, input, accounting and resource review, evaluate the three fixed checkpoints on at most 20 genuinely new, prospectively sampled paired tasks. The planned limit under horizon 50 is 3,000 generations and 3,060 reset/step operations for evaluation, plus separately bounded acquisition and training; unknown or incomplete physical work is charged conservatively, not classified as zero. Predeclare all continuation criteria, including data yield and acceptable technical failure/incomplete-evidence rates, before looking at outcomes. Any actual outcome is descriptive and this stage cannot establish efficacy.
4. **Confirmatory decision.** Consider a fresh independent sample and a preregistered inferential plan only if feasibility and the complete budget justify it. The main prospective contrast is C4−P0; C4−C1 is the second prespecified contrast, with simultaneous 95% coverage. A candidate scientifically meaningful success criterion is a familywise lower confidence bound of at least +5 percentage points for C4−P0, together with valid data provenance, no broken blinding or isolation, and complete technical accounting. This threshold is a proposed decision rule, not a claim that it is economically justified or powered. Report C4−C1 and uncertainty irrespective of its direction; do not require it to be positive in order to claim only the distinct C4−P0 contrast. Stop for an upper bound at or below zero; otherwise preserve an inconclusive outcome unless a separately predeclared continuation rule applies.

For illustration, two simultaneous distribution-free Hoeffding intervals with worst-case half-width about 5 points require 3,506 independent paired task draws and, at three arms and 50 turns, a ceiling of 525,900 generations and 536,418 reset/step operations for evaluation alone. These figures are neither a required universal design nor an approved or complete budget; training, token totals, GPU time, task generation/validation and unknown failures are additional. A +5-point lower-bound requirement with a ±5-point interval generally needs a point estimate at least +10 points; it does not guarantee detection of a true +5-point improvement. Consider more efficient prespecified paired analyses only with justified assumptions, actual discordance estimates and a credible complete budget. The historical 5.56-point R19 oracle quantity is a different estimand and cannot be treated as the predicted C4−P0 treatment effect.

Task draws must be independent under the declared generator and validation procedure; fixed-family claims do not become cross-family generalization by drawing more variants. Preserve the prior search-scope incident, all original evidence and the unknown status of historical and model-pretraining exposure.
