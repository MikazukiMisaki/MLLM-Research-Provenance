# Independent review of three-arm development preregistration v2

**Decision: conceptually coherent conditional design; NOT YET a complete frozen preregistration and NOT execution-ready.** v2 addresses the seven prior review categories in its written design. The remaining open issues concern actual frozen bytes/cases, production wiring and finite measurement, not evidence of a failed new experiment. Reviewing the method now does not prevent documenting the candidate, but a pre-outcome final protocol must bind these open choices before it can be called preregistered or run.

## Prior issues: status in v2

1. **P0 vs H4 interface:** addressed at the conceptual level. Draft specifies SFT-P0 LoRA actor, 16-token cap and final-nonempty-line `parse_p2_selection`, distinct from H4 actor/32 and unadapted Qwen recovery stages/40 and 16. It notes P0's shown parser lacks the recovery-style output-completeness gate. The exact base and adapter hashes, tokenizer, executable P0 adapter/backend and loading/device binding remain pending. The frozen H4 constructor configuration required by the historical recovery operator is not a claim that SFT-P0 is itself that backend.
2. **Causal contrast/status bit:** addressed in interpretation: A is an unequal-cost P0 baseline; B–C compares diagnosis/subgoal text to placeholders while status is visible. Valid/invalid affects the marker and handback; it is not full first-stage information removal or matched FLOPs. Distinct outputs/statuses from repeated greedy generation are retained rather than excluded, as long as no technical contract failed. Actual masking and lack of leakage into P0 still need an implementation, not only a design statement.
3. **Stage-1 semantic flag:** draft's new per-field TRUE/FALSE/UNKNOWN and aggregate rule preserve original historical parser decisions and gate behavior. This addresses the interpretation concern on paper; source `matched_extra.py` in the independently reviewed fake fixture still logs a single `semantic_nonempty` Boolean, including True for some truncated strings and False for missing values. Its fake PASS must not be presented as implementation or validation of v2's tri-state. Any implementation must store original raw/status/completeness plus the separate tri-state and fail-stop on recording errors, with independent synthetic tests. An empty diagnosis historically classified valid remains valid for execution under this draft; a stricter gate is a different prospectively versioned treatment.
4. **Fixed prefix and branch provenance:** v2 fixes `r=1` before results, preserves source A, replays for B/C, alternates their order, leaves hidden state UNKNOWN, and does not replace source-terminal or actor-withheld groups. It explicitly distinguishes withheld, terminal and technical UNKNOWN; future executable code still needs byte-bound replay/current ordered map/request/goal and a deterministic event ledger. Source-first ordering remains a limitation and four separate self-authored ancestries and custody remain unverified.
5. **Invalid / UNKNOWN:** one prospective OSR-style handback is selected symmetrically for complete ordinary invalid stage outputs; recording failures, exceptions, map ambiguity or incomplete operations lead to technical STOP/UNKNOWN and no retry. Preserve that ambiguity rule for source P0 as well: a complete valid but ambiguous environment mapping is not silently recoded as an ordinary withheld parser failure. A missing action or terminal before first action must be distinguished by what is actually observable, otherwise UNKNOWN. Future code/tests must show these paths, including P0 receipt failures and resumed history excluding private control text.
6. **Horizon and count:** the stated assumptions yield r+a+c≤50, a=1 implies c≤48, a=0 implies c≤49. With four source episodes and eight replay branches: 12 reset starts, ≤600 step starts, 592 P0 + 8 stage-1 + 8 stage-2 = 608 generation starts. Under P0=16, Stage1=40, Stage2=16, max output = 592×16+8×40+8×16 = 9,920 tokens. Under an enforced actual-input cap 4096, max input = 608×4096 = 2,490,368. These are conservative, separate maxima conditional on implementation; outcomes/terminals reduce realized counts. They exclude loads, adapter attachment, CPU tokenization/template, compiler/translation, descendants, prior work, failures/unknown tails and cleanup and hence do not constitute a complete physical budget. For the exact bound, verify P0 decoding cap 16 and actual generation tensor IDs rather than only pretokenizer text.
7. **Learning objective / SI-U1:** addressed in section 6: this protocol has no training/evaluation of an updated agent and can at most generate candidate corrective state/action/successor experiences. Success after the intervention cannot by itself label the action beneficial since subsequent P0 behavior is a confound. At most four development cases do not establish data sufficiency, independence, statistical precision, or success improvement; separate label quality, equal-quality/exposure controls, lineage, training and task-isolated post-training comparison are required.

## Still unresolved before final preregistration and execution

- The chosen C placeholder strings remain labeled proposals (`No diagnosis provided.` / `No subgoal provided.`). The existing matched-extra synthetic fixture instead uses `(no diagnostic information)` twice. An ablation's prompt bytes affect behavior and cost: choose exact Unicode/whitespace and prompt-rendering version prospectively, then independently test that same exact bytes reach the actual tokenizer input; do not claim the existing fixture validated the proposed strings. Its current flow is `REJECTED_NO_CONTINUATION` / `STEP_RETURNED_NO_CONTINUATION`, not P0 handback or three-arm execution.
- Fix and hash the four eligible self-authored task identities, ancestry/near-duplicate criteria and order before observing results; reserve them without scanning historical or protected pools. Specify initialization/version, verification and output retention. A declaration/metadata checker cannot itself prove custody or absence of unknown historical overlap. If four qualifying groups cannot be established, record infeasibility rather than opening protected assets or replacing them.
- Specify immutable actual P0/recovery code, model, tokenizer, adapter and source/environment/config; deterministic request/map and prefix replay; raw operation starts/returns/UNKNOWN; exact actor output and no-action handling; source-run completion and A reuse; and independently reviewed no-retry supervisor. Model-load count 2 is a conditional implementation proposal, while adapter load/attach is an additional operation. No real model fit, total budget, cumulative-cost reconciliation, wall/deadline, resource/descendant accounting or protected-data boundary is demonstrated by the draft.
- Independently test the causal and safety boundaries: content-private matched output must not enter P0 history/observation or later prompts; status bit remains intentionally available; all three Stage-1 tri-state nonempty indicators must apply without rewriting frozen parser labels; exact two recovery starts on ordinary stage-1 invalid; logging/failed-step UNKNOWN and no-retry; actual P0 parser and cap; duplicate/stale mapping; reset/replay `r=1`, source natural continuation reuse and horizon. Test this with fake model/environment edges first; production tokenizer/model/ALFWorld behavior is not established by that test.
- Finalize full per-operation numeric budgets, resource admission/monitor/timeout and incomplete-operation reconciliation, statistical/descriptive reporting, paired case order, no-replacement and source outcome-blinding procedures. The draft already acknowledges that four cases cannot establish a meaningful success-rate gain or absence of harm; do not upgrade the gate to a positive scientific effectiveness claim.

The exact fixed-placeholder decision and tri-state implementation are immediate low-risk next work; they do not require a real model or environment. No action or release follows from this review.
