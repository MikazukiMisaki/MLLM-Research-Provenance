# Independent review of 20-draw feasibility budget

Decision: **reasonable planning basis, not frozen and not ready to execute**. The draft distinguishes 20 paired task draws from scientific confirmation and explicitly separates engineering probes, task authoring, acquisition, training, evaluation, incomplete work and prior costs. I independently checked the proposed 60-episode horizon-50 bounds: at most 3,000 action-decision generations and 3,060 reset-plus-step operations only when the stated one-call-per-decision and single-reset rules actually hold. Other calls, failed in-flight work, compiles, model loads and training are not covered by those two figures.

The operational ten-distinct-tasks-per-family screening floor is a design choice, not a statistical representativeness guarantee or evidence that enough independent task families exist. Task and environment-seed lineage, a sealed independently custodied future confirmatory support and a verified common-pool source remain prerequisites. A 50/50 within-family pilot uses an equal-stratum target; changing the deployment mixture requires explicit weights. No estimator should silently generalize to new task families or training runs.

One shared environment seed across arms can improve pairing only if actual runtime semantics support it; treatment-dependent random-number consumption may make later randomness diverge. A successful paired pilot must therefore preserve actual reset identities and disclose residual randomness, not promise identical future transitions. A technical stop may depend on outcomes, so complete-run evidence cannot automatically claim conditional coverage. Track unknown external operations separately, with reviewed conservative bounds where available.

The draft does not fix the presently unknown costs of authoring, collection, corrections, two real training arms, model fit, evaluation, telemetry and abort tails. Its formulas are not numeric overall limits and confer no execution release. No empirical C4 success result, independent confirmatory support or production real-data weighted trainer is established by this review. The next actionable technical step is a separately bounded, independently reviewed engineering probe; passing it would establish a limited interface fact, not readiness of the whole feasibility study.
