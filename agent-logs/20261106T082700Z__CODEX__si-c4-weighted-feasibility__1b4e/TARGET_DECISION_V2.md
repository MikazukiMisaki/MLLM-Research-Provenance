## Bounded value arithmetic

Compare a hypothetical new product with the historically observed C product on precisely the same 82 already-exposed tasks. Assume the eight previously failed pick-two outcomes improve by g and that all other 74 outcomes do not change. Then the overall observed panel improvement is exactly `g/82`: one gain gives 1.22 percentage points; two 2.44; four 4.88; all eight 9.76. Under this explicit no-other-change assumption 9.76 pp is the maximal contribution of those eight tasks, not a bound on total C4 change or future effects. Regressions in the other 74 reduce or can reverse the net result; e.g. four target gains and four other losses give zero overall change. A new C1 product may itself differ from historic C, and no same-task comparability has been established for another run.

For an illustrative +5 pp net threshold on exactly 82 episodes, at least five net gains are required (5/82 ≈ 6.10 pp). This is arithmetic only: neither 5 pp nor a corresponding loss criterion is established as practically worthwhile, and repeated tuning on the exposed panel would bias selection. An acceptable threshold must be justified against evaluation/training costs and alternative actions in advance, with fixed handling of all arms, missing results and regressions; it is not evidence of improved generalization or a plan to cherry-pick tasks. Under the frozen evaluator an invalid unselected action yields a failed episode, while technical errors, truncation and evidence drift leave comparison incomplete.

Illustratively, zero successes in eight independently sampled homogeneous Bernoulli tasks would give a one-sided 95% exact upper bound of 31.23% on that hypothetical success probability. The actual eight are an outcome-reviewed, fixed and potentially correlated family subset, with unknown population-sampling mechanism; this bound is not a valid claim about real family performance. It shows why eight failures cannot establish a precise population rate even under ideal conditions.

## Decision path

Proceed with software-only preparation of a separate C1/C4 production interface and its budget/measurement control only if it is directly connected to a prospective lawful use, independently testable with invented fixtures, and does not modify historical B/C semantics. Independently validate same-data/same-order C1 equivalence, shifted-token loss and gradient normalization for C4, clean separation of gate schemas, failure and replay controls. Synthetic GPT2/Qwen fixtures do not prove actual full-sized Qwen BF16/CUDA execution.

Before any real experiment, separately establish a legitimately accessible source and exact permitted use, independently validate labeling/lineage and task isolation for the intended claim, prospectively choose a decision-changing effect/guardrail and feasible precision, and establish full physical budgets including unknown tails. If these are not evidenced, stop at software preparation; do not reclassify existing 82 tasks as fresh confirmation, infer source causality from different data, or generate results from protected assets. The user's general instruction to continue is already given; these are evidence requirements, not an invented request for renewed general permission.
