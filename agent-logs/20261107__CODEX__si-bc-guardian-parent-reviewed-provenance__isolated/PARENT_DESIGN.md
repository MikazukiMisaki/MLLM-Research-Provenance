# Minimum-cost route to independent same-domain parents

**Decision: proceed with design and a bounded, outcome-blind feasibility study, not a confirmatory sample claim.** No model, ALFWorld, task, seed, protected identity or witness was opened, generated or executed for this note. No independent custodian or large fresh pool has been established. A workable generator may not be able to supply the desired number of structurally distinct, valid parents; this is an empirical feasibility question rather than a solved asset problem.

## Define what counts as a different parent

Before generating candidates, freeze a versioned **parent** as a distinct goal-relevant causal structure: a normalized relational description of goal predicates, necessary action/operator types and precedence, object roles/types, relevant initial-state dependencies, obstacles and invariants. Alpha-renaming identifiers, changing filename, seed, prose, arrangement that leaves causal structure unchanged, or merely resampling an existing initial state gives variants of one parent, not a new independent parent. A state detail counts toward a distinct structure only under a preregistered rule that it changes necessary reachable transitions or dependencies; a symbolic graph difference alone is not proof of semantic difference. This is intentionally conservative and may collapse many seemingly different generated instances.

Implement an allowlisted normalizer and exact digest over canonical operator/goal/initial relational graphs, plus declared provenance/ancestor IDs and graph closure. Check graph isomorphism under permitted renaming; flag near-matches and common generators/templates for independent manual causal review. Treat undecidable relationships, missing ancestor records, unknown external roots and cycles as UNKNOWN. Document false merges/splits with **fabricated** examples. Do not infer uniqueness from count of seeds or random draws, task names, or HMAC commitments. Where graphs cannot capture a shared family mechanism, treat parents as potentially clustered. Independent parent structures are not automatically statistically independent when drawn from a few related templates; preregister a parent/family hierarchy, generation law and clustered or family-sensitive analysis. Distinct manufactured tasks support inference about the fixed generated distribution only, not the whole ALFWorld benchmark or new environments.

To control leakage, a separate custodian must compare the frozen candidate-parent inventory against the available historical-use inventory and exclusions without revealing task names, contents, witness plans, seed values or per-candidate collision answers to the research team. Exact and normalized checks plus bounded manual review are conditional on historical inventory completeness. Unknown past exposure, missing inventory, undocumented model pretraining or ambiguous ancestry cannot be proved away. A commitment proves later byte consistency, not that a task was unseen or that the manifest was honest.

## Smallest concrete preparation and feasibility proposal

1. **Public design, no hidden task access:** inventory only permitted public domain operator/grammar interfaces and record version/hashes. Specify a finite, broad proposal space of dependency graphs and task families, constraints, allowed initial states, rejection criteria, unique-parent definition, source lineage, selection distribution and fixed family allocation. Freeze and independently review the generator, canonicalizer, validity checks and recording protocol. Candidate two pre-existing templates alone are insufficient evidence of many independent parent structures; no claim about parent supply is made here.
2. **Fabricated-only software checks:** unit-test graph alpha-renaming, isomorphism, duplicated/related parents, transitive ancestors, missing lineage, contradictory facts, invalid/nonfinite or duplicate-key records, an unresolvable overlap and a masked witness. Confirm none of the public summary, actor request, exception or ordinary logs emits protected contents or a lookup oracle. Tests use invented miniature fixtures and do not inspect actual protected material. Enforceability of source/package closure and process isolation must be independently assessed rather than asserted by these tests.
3. **Custody feasibility:** obtain an actual separate account or independent human/process with restricted storage, independently administered credentials and auditable access. The custodian freezes secure randomness and parent inventory and retains per-task graph, generated task and witness; coordinator receives only a prespecified aggregate pass/fail/UNKNOWN report. On a shared UID or filesystem, same-account agents are not independent custodians. If such a boundary and historical-use overlap check cannot be demonstrated, label any resulting set `CUSTODY_UNVERIFIED_SHARED_ACCESS` and use it only as explicitly exposed exploratory development, not confirmation. Do not open an existing protected set to repair this gap.
4. **One bounded parent-supply pilot, no policy outcomes:** after review and separately defined authorization for these task-construction/validation actions, permit at most **48 fixed-order generation proposals**, target **8 accepted distinct parent structures**, with a frozen target-family allocation. Reject invalid/duplicate/ambiguous candidates regardless of policy quality. Use deterministic static syntax/type/invariant checks; if an approved local non-model planner is available, generate witnesses independently and keep them restricted. Validity/reachability and witness availability are unknown until actually checked; lack of a planner is a feasibility stop, not permission to consult a model or expose protected data. For actual environment validation, cap at **16 environment constructions, 16 resets, 800 action steps, 16 closes**, no policy-model calls and no training/evaluation. A witness replay has at most 50 steps per reset. Treat counts as separate caps, not a claim of zero other work; meter parsing, planner operations, CPU, wall time, disk, rejected attempts and failures separately, and retain raw costs. No GPU work is planned; actual native dependencies or incidental resource use must be measured, not assumed zero. Set CPU/wall/storage ceilings only after measuring a purely fabricated setup; do not invent an enforceable ceiling here.
5. **Accept or stop:** accept the pilot as an engineering feasibility result only if the preregistered independent reviewer verifies 8 valid, pairwise distinct parent structures, correct sealed lineage and zero known cross-partition collisions, with no unresolved equivalence or custody/access failure. A technical stop, failed or inaccessible witness, unsupported environment action, partial cost, unknown parent relation or exhausted cap means preserve evidence and `INSUFFICIENT_OR_UNKNOWN`; no automatic retry, model-selected replacement or outcome-driven backfill. Eight is not a power demonstration. If the parent yield or verification throughput makes 80–200 independent parents implausible within resources, stop confirmation planning and continue only clearly labeled exploratory research or seek a separately sourced asset.

## Blind holdout and independent verification

Only after generator and methods are frozen, the custodian samples separate development/acquisition, feasibility and final-confirmation supports using independent private streams, checks exact and transitive parent disjointness, and performs the frozen historical-overlap and causal review. Reserve confirmation parents that are not exercised in pilot/feasibility; any change to generator, selection, treatment, endpoint or analysis after inspecting feasibility outcomes requires an appropriate fresh frozen confirmation support. An 8-parent pilot supplies evidence about the generation process but cannot double as the final unseen evaluation sample.

For each partition, bind the complete canonical manifest, generator and validator hashes, environment versions, validity rules, family allocation, arm order and privately generated randomness with a versioned, domain-separated HMAC using a high-entropy custodian-held secret. Anchor the aggregate commitment and custody attestation outside the custodian's editable storage before outcomes; separately verify account permissions and access history. Raw unsalted hashes of low-entropy task/seed content may enable guessing and should not be public. Reveal only after the prespecified stage under controlled audit rules. This binds later openings to earlier bytes, but cannot establish no prior disclosure, no pretraining exposure, or an honestly sampled support. Avoid per-candidate collision lookups, task-level validator output or repeated adaptive overlap queries, all of which can leak membership.

For actual model runs, a controlled owner supplies only each task's minimum environment material; keep witnesses inaccessible to the actor and inspect that public prompts and trajectories contain no hidden verifier/witness information. Shared-UID access, policy/environment co-residence and native libraries may prevent a strong confidentiality claim even if filenames are omitted. If runtime secrecy or existing exposure cannot be independently established, the evidence remains exploratory.

## Sample precision and complete budget

For the single primary paired endpoint define `n10` (C-low success/P0 failure) and `n01` (P0 success/C-low failure), report `(n10-n01)/N` and all four paired cells; preregister a defensible paired 95% interval, an exact two-sided conditional test, missing/technical UNKNOWN handling, meaningful effect and any family or cluster analysis. The referenced safe calculation estimates about **92** independent parents for 80% power under the purely hypothetical +15-point effect/25% discordance, and **168** under +10 points/20% discordance; neither effect is an observed estimate, and power is not guaranteed under dependence or cluster sampling. If parent/family independence cannot be justified, naive parent counts can overstate precision. Do not selectively discard unknowns; prespecify sensitivity bounds.

For a minimal **two-arm** confirmation campaign with N parents and no retries, the structural maxima are `2N` episodes, `100N` generations, `102N` environment resets plus action steps, `409,600N` input tokens and `1,600N` output tokens, plus up to `2N` constructions/loads/closes and all validation, hashing, failed-start and audit costs. At N=120 this is 240 episodes, 12,000 generations and 12,240 reset/step operations; at N=168 it is 336 episodes, 16,800 generations and 17,136 operations. Input maxima are 49,152,000 and 68,812,800 tokens respectively; output maxima 192,000 and 268,800. Adding C-high requires another N episodes and its full budget, not free secondary analysis.

Using only the conditional baseline from the safe summary—one full five-shard logical hash and one equally sized logical model-load allowance per episode, five-shard size 16,381,516,776 bytes—the two-arm planning expression is `4N × 16,381,516,776` logical bytes: approximately **7,323.1 GiB** at N=120 or **10,252.4 GiB** at N=168. These are estimates, not measured physical disk reads or a hard quota, and exclude adapter/package bytes, output, environment assets, audits, retries and prior work. Actual model-load read amounts and any persistent-model alternative require separate measurement, safety review and clean cross-episode state; do not silently assume reuse. Sum all prior train, GPU and failed-operation costs with future work exactly once; include one-shot failure/UNKNOWN, setup, cleanup, wall/GPU allocation, I/O, storage and possible sample overshoot. If complete budgets or resource ownership cannot be justified, do not declare readiness for confirmation.
