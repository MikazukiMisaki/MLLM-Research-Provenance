# Independent review: minimal self-improvement feasibility

Status: DESIGN_ONLY. No historical task content, unknown task inventory, model or environment was accessed or run for this review. Inputs were the sanitized same-domain semantic-family design, topology-isolation sensitivity and engineering budget bridge. All thresholds and budgets below are proposals, not observed yields or authorized claims.

## Decision

Proceed in stages toward a tightly bounded same-domain *feasibility* study, beginning with verified engineering and a fixed, training-side acquisition protocol. A self-authored generator can support a documented comparison on a clearly specified constructed distribution if production compatibility and split custody are established. It does **not** by itself establish non-overlap with unknown historical tasks, absence of pretraining exposure, or broad ALFWorld generalization. The existing abstract 12-cell grid cannot claim component isolation under the reasonable one-edit adjacency in the sensitivity note. The proposed `(family, goal cardinality)` merging rule yields only two evaluation components, so replicated variants within those components do not provide across-component statistical precision.

The primary goal is success-rate improvement through training on collected experience, not merely learning the synthetic label or producing a working parser. A 3-case environment check remains engineering evidence only. One training product per arm establishes neither training-run stability nor expected training-algorithm effect.

## Minimal operational design

1. Freeze the public-domain generator code, domain version, role-labelled canonical structure, valid goal and natural-language instruction, as well as the rules for train-family development. Verify goal-not-initially-satisfied, reachability, action naming, ambiguity, state persistence and actual production adapter compatibility. Fix a conservative connected-component/lineage rule before any outcomes; assign shared concrete parents and linked descendants to one side. Document exact overlap with the expressly authorized self-authored generation records; do not claim historical or pretraining disjointness from unavailable provenance. Generation-code commonality alone is not automatic leakage, but it limits the transfer claim.
2. Reserve evaluation by a frozen generator specification and sealed seed/instance assignment under documented separate custody before training-side collection. No model-generated evaluation data, evaluation results or post-hoc eval edits enter acquisition, selection or tuning. A separate execution identity or agent is operational separation, not proof of a statistically independent population; keep an append-only commitment and audit trail. Predeclare the estimand: finite constructed set or one-to-two-object compositional transfer, not IID ALFWorld benchmark performance. Inability to guarantee new evaluation material remains explicit rather than searching protected assets.
3. Fix P0, prompts, decoding, horizon, admissible action and parser policy, episode ordering and maximum attempts before any outcomes. Sample only permitted training instances; retain every start, completion, failed/invalid action and cost. Partition training tasks by frozen lineage before collecting; never include evaluation episodes, source states, traces or their descendants in training. Per-instance P0 failure is observational evidence, not a training target by itself.
4. Define a fixed failure classification based only on these training-side P0 records, with blinded-to-treatment human review or an independently tested deterministic rule, explicit ambiguous/missing category, and no outcome-dependent post-comparison relabelling. For eligible states use one prespecified bounded intervention, replaying the recorded training prefix and continuing under fixed rules. Preserve all unsuccessful, invalid, incomplete and discarded attempts. Demonstrate success under the actual environment before using a corrected action as a positive supervised target; do not train the failure action as successful. If corrected examples are unavailable or unsuitable, feasibility is NOT_ESTABLISHED, not a manufactured label. Training labels are observations on permitted training experience; self-authored expert demonstrations, if used, are separately identified and cannot be presented as self-collected.
5. Freeze a common, quality-filtered set of distinct loss examples and common ordering, starting checkpoint and total training exposure for both arms. Define M and K at loss-example granularity, require `0 < K < M`, report task/component/label counts and active-token distributions, and use C1 weight 1 vs C4 weights `4*M/(M+3*K)` for target and `M/(M+3*K)` for others under the independently checked token-normalized update contract. The arm difference includes optimizer/clipping scale and therefore is the full weighted recipe. No adjustment based on final evaluation. A tiny viability gate, e.g. at least 4 deduplicated usable loss examples in each label with multiple training source tasks, is an engineering/data-yield threshold, not a powered scientific sample or proof of effectiveness; insufficient yield means stop or preregister a new bounded acquisition proposal.
6. Before first science execution, preregister all contrasts, success criteria, failures, fixed seeds and component assignments, and budget. Freeze the trained products before evaluation. Compare P0, C1 and C4 on the same eligible evaluation units under the same horizon and parser. Retain paired outcomes and all errors. Report finite-set arm success counts and paired wins/losses, by family/component, including denominator. Replicate at least three separately seeded C1 and C4 products if resources permit; three is a preliminary sensitivity check and cannot yield precise product-distribution estimates. Model stochasticity, task correlation and training-seed variability are separate sources of uncertainty.

## Statistical interpretation and stop criteria

Predeclare the smallest relevant improvement, e.g. +10 percentage points paired success, and a desired 95% interval half-width of at most 10 points; these are planning values, not results. Under independent identically sampled bounded *paired units* with range [-1,1], a two-sided distribution-free Hoeffding bound requires at least `ceil(2*ln(40)/0.1^2) = 738` such units for that half-width. This is a conservative sufficient bound, not a universal sample requirement or guarantee under dependent selected tasks. A normal approximation with prespecified paired discordance 0.20 and near-zero difference gives about 77 independent paired units for a 10-point half-width; actual discordance is unknown and the approximation is neither a guarantee nor a power analysis. Repeated variants from two coarse components are not 77 independent components. At 20 independent units the distribution-free bound is about ±60.7 points; it cannot support a precise 10-point claim. With a fixed finite set, present its exact paired contrast as conditional, without assigning population confidence bounds absent a sampling/randomization design.

Stop acquisition on malformed state/action mapping, parser inconsistency, invalid reachability, any split/lineage conflict, unexpected mutation of inputs, unknown physical operation, budget breach or unobserved process state. Stop the data stage if fixed minimum quality/yield is unmet; do not expand or alter selection based on apparent treatment effect. Stop an efficacy claim if the task sampling/custody and analysis do not justify it, regardless of directional finite-set improvement. Preserve evidence, costs and failed attempts; no automatic retry or backfill outside a separately frozen policy.

## Illustrative budget envelope, not a release or forecast

The actual engineering witness recorded one construct, one load, one reset and three steps, zero model calls. The separate next proposed 3-case check has conditional maximum three constructs, three loads, three resets and 13 steps, zero model calls; this is not training budget. Prior SI smoke's physical GPU time is unknown and must remain unknown. Avoid silently combining R19 and SI campaign totals.

For a concrete prospective feasibility ceiling, suppose at most 30 training P0 episodes, horizon 50 and at most one model generation per attempted source step, then at most 10 one-step intervention branches. If each branch reconstructs at most 49 prior steps on a fresh reset, uses one generated intervention action and permits at most 49 subsequent P0-generated steps, and no retries/extra model prompts occur:

* acquisition: at most 30 resets, 1,500 steps and 1,500 generations;
* branches: at most 10 resets, 990 steps (490 replay + 10 intervention + 490 continuation), and 500 generations (10 intervention + 490 continuation);
* total: at most 40 resets + 2,490 steps = 2,530 reset-plus-step operations, and 2,000 generations. Charge every completed, failed and uncertain started operation; constructs, loads, startup, transcription, possible discarded output and internal translations are separate. These are upper bounds conditional on the stated policy, not assertions about realized calls or dataset yield.

For an illustrative 20-evaluation-unit conditional study with one P0 plus three C1 and three C4 products, one reset and at most 50 generated steps per product/unit gives an additional cap of 140 resets, 7,000 steps and 7,000 generations, before engineering, validation and cleanup. Thus acquisition plus that evaluation alone could reach 180 resets, 9,490 steps (9,670 operations) and 9,000 generations; no training cost is included. Twenty variants are not twenty independent components unless the frozen grouping and sampling justify it.

The complete budget remains UNRESOLVED: checkpoint and model loads; active and total prompt/output token ceilings including growing context; LoRA/optimizer state and device memory; preprocessing, epochs, distinct loss examples, training forward/backward/update counts; seeds, validation and model saving; interpreter/ALFWorld construction and replay translations; explicit wall-clock/device-hour ceilings and measurement, shared-GPU contention and ownership; disk, host RAM, monitor sampling/cleanup tails and failed/incomplete/unknown external operations. Before training, bind actual model and adapter identities, tokenizer, token limits, batches/accumulation, update count, hardware admission rules, per-stage stop conditions and a single aggregate ceiling; feasibility cannot be inferred from an idle GPU snapshot. Live cost must use actual logs, not these illustrative maxima.

## Blocking unknowns

* Actual executable self-authored families and production instructions/action mappings, and a justified connected-component split with enough independent evaluation units.
* Historical/pretraining overlap and licensed or documented data-use provenance: UNKNOWN, not absence; the resulting generalization claim must be narrowed accordingly.
* Training-side yield and unambiguous, genuinely corrected success targets; 0<K<M and viable distinct example counts are unproven.
* Fixed effect threshold, sampling/estimand, independent-unit count and training-run variability; full physical and monetary budget and shared GPU availability are unmeasured.

Prepared as an independent local scientific design review; it does not itself authorize an efficacy claim or imply that a model/ALFWorld run occurred.
