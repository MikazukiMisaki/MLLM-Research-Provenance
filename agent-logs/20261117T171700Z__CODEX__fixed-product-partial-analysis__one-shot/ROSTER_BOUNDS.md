# Full planned-roster missingness bounds

For the four prespecified DEV cases, P0 has one observed success, two observed failures, and one not-run outcome; its four-case descriptive success fraction can range from 1/4 to 2/4 depending on the missing outcome. The candidate has one observed success, one observed failure, and two unknown/not-run outcomes; its fraction can range from 1/4 to 3/4. Because P0 on case c is known to have failed, the candidate-minus-P0 difference over the full four-case roster ranges from -1/4 to +2/4. These are worst-case logical bounds for missing binary outcomes, not confidence intervals or predicted effects. The incomplete candidate arm and absent case d are not imputed into the observed result. Previously used, related task variants provide development observations rather than independent confirmation.
