## Concrete blocking limitation for a real binding claim

The acknowledged post-check race is reproducible without threads, CUDA or malicious native code. `prompt_tensor_binding_fake.py:98–101` hashes the tensor and calls the existing `GenerationGate`. The latter then calls its supplied `generation_started` emitter before invoking the model (`production_episode.py:52–54`). The independent test `test_post_digest_gate_emit_mutation_reaches_fake_model` uses a synthetic emitter that mutates the same input tensor at that point. The fake model receives a tensor with a digest different from the digest already passed to the reservation callback, and the call succeeds. This does **not** show the actual emitter does mutate inputs and is within the candidate's declared race limitation; it does show the proposed digest cannot yet be described as a guaranteed digest of the actual generation input. Resolve or narrow the claim and independently review the effective generation boundary before any real release; account for non-atomic/concurrent device mutation even after adding checks.

## Other conditions and limits

- The tokenizer and template implementation are trusted. The guard confirms their inputs and observed outputs, not that they implement a faithful tokenizer or that the model process cannot see extra same-process data. The prompt filter searches a finite list of textual markers; it is not a confidentiality boundary or complete hidden-state detector.
- Reservation here is a caller-provided synthetic callback, with no cross-process authenticity, durability or binding to a real cost ledger. It occurs before generation-configuration validation. Any accepted real reservation must be retained and charged/marked unresolved if a later config check, mutation check, child failure or journal failure stops before the underlying generation. The prototype's callback is not itself proof of reserve-before-effect.
- CPU copies and hashing occur repeatedly and can synchronize device work. Their overhead, limits and effects have not been measured or budgeted on real inputs. Same-UID source replacement, stale bytecode, model-loading/import provenance, multi-unit sequencing, descendant cleanup, cumulative budget, task isolation and the C-low prior outer UNKNOWN require separate review.
- Hashes alone do not establish independent custody, protection from guessing low-entropy prompts, or semantic alignment of public state and prompt. Save sensitive per-event hashes and raw logs according to their actual privacy classification. Production event handling outside the reservation callback can still record prompt text and must be reviewed separately.

This review changes no scientific source, consumes no scientific unit and authorizes no preflight, model call or execution. No treatment efficacy is inferred.
