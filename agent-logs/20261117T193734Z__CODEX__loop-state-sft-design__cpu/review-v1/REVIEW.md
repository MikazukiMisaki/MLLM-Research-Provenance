## What the contrast can and cannot answer

Selecting one matched target/control state from the same P0 successful episode while otherwise fixing training setup is an appropriate way to study whether *the selection policy for SFT states* changes downstream success relative to another selection policy. Both states are from the same source; the experiment cannot estimate a self-versus-expert/source effect. The target and control deliberately differ in state, history and location within episode. A treatment difference would concern the complete state-selection/data-construction policy, not prove that the loop pattern itself caused the difference or that any particular action broke a failure loop. The proposal appropriately avoids claiming that its post-loop action is causal, or that same-episode success proves recovery from failure.

Success-only episode selection conditions on an outcome, excluding failed P0 episodes and all parents without a qualifying match. The resulting intervention and any effectiveness estimate apply to this selected corpus, not to all parents or failed trajectories. Within-episode matching reduces source/parent/action differences, but cannot equalize unobserved state difficulty, state position, length, prior history or the relevance of the same command. Record predeclared selection/exclusion denominators and training parents, and describe applicability accordingly. Frozen P0 is an informative no-update reference, but one descriptive baseline does not itself isolate source, selection, or mechanism.

## Required fixes before freezing an executable protocol

1. **Label equality.** The opening claims identical supervised token labels, but selection rule 2 matches only command and Action-ID, and rule 4 matches group-level *counts*. Neither entails identical raw output or the exact target token ID sequence, special tokens and loss mask. Require pairwise equality of the complete training label IDs and loss masks under a frozen tokenizer/template (reject instead of modifying originals), or weaken the hypothesis to acknowledge a coupled label difference and separately report it. Matching full labels may make feasibility lower; assess it as predeclared feasibility, not a post-result relaxation. The fictional example has no output token IDs or loss masks and cannot prove this check.
2. **Full training context.** A maximum 4096 input IDs plus 16 target IDs can require 4112 positions, possibly more with template/special tokens. Bind actual tokenizer/trainer formatting, maximum model context and loss-mask semantics; count the final tokenized training sequence, reject any over the actual total supported length, and preserve separate input/target ceilings. Do not rely on component ceilings to establish combined feasibility or silently truncate. Verify token counts and padding/packing, EOS, prompt masking, gradients and example weighting in the actual chosen trainer before execution.
3. **Corpus and task isolation.** Before inspecting outcome patterns, freeze source policy identity, allowed acquisition, candidate roster and ordering, first eligible success definition, matching/normalization, stop rule, and all exclusion reasons. Clarify whether exactly 16 preselected training parents must each yield a match or whether a larger finite predeclared candidate roster may be screened to find the first 16; neither post hoc replacement nor searching additional episodes is allowed under the current stated rule. Define a durable parent identity and lineage, including task families/templates/variants and prior use, then fix parent-disjoint evaluation roster independently of training selection. Hash inequality alone does not establish independence or absence of overlapping variants. Unknown source or exposure is not an independent confirmatory set; label exposed DEV as exploratory. Do not invent or silently substitute unavailable assets.
4. **Statistics and estimand.** Specify the primary estimand as the average over the 16 prespecified evaluation parents of the within-parent difference in mean success over three *paired training seeds*, conditional on the selected training corpus and fixed evaluation protocol. Fix the seed values, per-parent evaluation conditions and arm/example ordering before outcomes. Six trained products and 16 repeated parent outcomes are not 48 independent parents. Prespecify a parent-clustered paired descriptive interval, its small-sample limitations and whether it is conditional on the observed training seeds; three seeds are insufficient to precisely infer variation across training runs. P0 comparison is descriptive, not a third confirmatory causal contrast. Any missing/unstarted/unknown arm episode remains UNKNOWN with a complete roster; stop and present paired-data completeness, not imputed zeroes or a successful inferential conclusion.
5. **Scientific decision.** Since this is a feasibility pilot with 16 parents, decide in advance which descriptive effect, uncertainty, feasibility and compute outcomes justify considering a separately powered follow-up, versus stopping or revising. Avoid an arbitrary post hoc significance threshold; neither this pilot nor 16 parents establishes reliable population benefit or a thesis-level claim by itself. If formal confirmation is later wanted, freeze an independently sourced parent roster, a prespecified estimand, multiplicity handling, meaningful effect and a justified power/precision design separately.
6. **Reproducible execution.** Freeze the exact P0 product, data-selection code, raw recorded labels, P0/control data schedules, all SFT hyperparameters and LoRA settings, held/fixed update count, seeds, checkpoint selection, task environment/decoding, and native outcome/unknown definitions before outcomes. Specify equal number of updates and loss weighting across arms; equal token totals alone cannot ensure identical per-example weighting. Do not use P0 or evaluation outcomes for tuning or checkpoint selection.

## Budget cross-check and unverified assumptions

Conditional on all 16 examples meeting bounds and no skipped microbatch: 16 examples × 4 epochs = 64 microbatches per training run; accumulation 4 gives 16 optimizer steps, and 6 runs give 384 microbatches / 96 steps. The specified component ceilings yield 384 × 4096 = 1,572,864 input IDs and 384 × 16 = 6,144 label IDs; this arithmetic is not a guarantee of valid combined context, memory use or loss computation.

P0 + six products × 16 parents gives 112 evaluation episodes. If each is a fresh process and the horizon limit applies as proposed, maxima 112 × 50 = 5,600 generations, 5,600 steps, 112 resets = 5,712 steps-plus-resets, and 22,937,600 input / 89,600 output IDs are arithmetically correct. Separate 112 materializations/constructions/initializations/loads are conservative process-level assumptions; training adds 6 product loads. Verify the actual process/effect mapping and persist all historical plus new physical costs, failures and UNKNOWNs. No inference about earlier cumulative cost follows from this read-only plan review.

The proposed four allocated GPU hours and six wall hours are **unmeasured stop limits**, not proof the planned six training runs, 112 episodes, loading and cleanup can finish or that shared-device admission is safe. No collection budget is allocated; viable recorded successful episodes with correct source identity and exact labels are unproven. Account explicitly for screening/I/O/CPU, six training loads and optimizer work, evaluation/runtime limits, failed/incomplete effects and cleanup. An unavailable source, insufficient eligible corpus, missing independent evaluation, inability to establish resource scope or a budget/identity mismatch stops feasibility; it cannot be converted into a research result.

The plan should remain DESIGN ONLY until the above protocol fields are fixed prospectively, actual inputs and execution chain are verified in their proper scope, and an independent implementation/launch review establishes the declared budgets.
