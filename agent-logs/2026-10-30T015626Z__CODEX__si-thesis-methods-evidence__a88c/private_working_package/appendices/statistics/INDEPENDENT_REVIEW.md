1. **Paired finite-schedule estimand and denominators:** each contrast's `wins`, `losses`, `ties`, and `(wins−losses)/n` agree with `paired_stats.py`; both-success and both-failure count as ties. The 82 task triples are paired by task and the 246 arm episodes are not 246 independent pairs. The corrected extractor's `check_schedule_rows` separately verifies full 82-task schedule and report identity/family/order/seed before supplying these pairs; the statistics function itself checks complete task/family sets and boolean outcomes, not order or seeds. This distinction is stated explicitly here for future implementation readers.
2. **Exact two-sided p and zero discordance:** the displayed doubled lower-tail binomial formula, capped at one, and the `d=0 → p=1` edge case exactly match the frozen pure statistics function; it is not a chi-square, continuity-corrected or mid-p procedure. Validity for a population interpretation depends on an appropriate null/sampling model for paired discordances, not merely the existence of a fixed schedule.
3. **Holm:** only C−A and C−B form this Holm family. The sorted two-p formula and mapping back to the source contrasts match the implementation. The procedure's familywise guarantee does not need mutual independence of the two tests, but does require valid marginal p-values. Two synthetic patterns tested both possible p-value orderings; an incomplete synthetic task list was rejected.
4. **Intervals:** for each of four win/loss counts, code computes a two-sided Clopper–Pearson component with `alpha=.0125`, denominator n, endpoint tails `.00625` and 100 floating-point bisections. The formula for x=0 / x=n edges and subtraction `[lower_win−upper_loss, upper_win−lower_loss]` is correct. The upper-end code solves the complementary survival-tail equation and is mathematically equivalent to the displayed `P(X≤x)=alpha/2` equation. Under valid component coverage, a union bound over four components gives simultaneous coverage of both difference intervals of at least 95%, without needing independence among those components. The union bound cannot compensate for invalid marginal sampling assumptions, and these intervals are neither a single CP difference interval nor Holm-test inversions.
5. **Observed values:** re-aggregating the 82 already-saved paired outcome records and independently calculating rational exact p-values and 70-digit Decimal component intervals reproduced 3/1/78 and 3/4/75, the two deltas, p=.625 and 1, Holm values 1 and 1, and both reported intervals to better than `1e-12` absolute tolerance. The recorded decision remains INCONCLUSIVE. No individual task ID, prompt, or outcome was emitted by this review.
6. **Interpretation:** the text correctly treats these as descriptive comparisons for fixed tasks/checkpoints. Population intervals/tests need a defensible task-generating model, such as suitable independent draws of paired outcomes; exchangeability or parent-directory uniqueness alone does not prove an independent binomial model. Within-task pairing does not resolve cross-task dependence, unrecorded prior exposure, observational pipeline differences, or one-seed training variability. p=1 and no warning do not demonstrate equivalence, nonharm or lack of an effect.
