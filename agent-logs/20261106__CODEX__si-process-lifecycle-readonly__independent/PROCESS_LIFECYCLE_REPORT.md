Status: **FEASIBILITY EVIDENCE ONLY; NOT A MODEL-EXECUTION CLEARANCE**. Inspection used this process's mount/credential information and two self-created CPU-only fake-process probes. No real model, ALFWorld, GPU query, CUDA context, other user's process details, shared service configuration, cgroup mutation or global setting was touched. Both probe subprocesses returned 0, and each test reports all of its self-created children reaped. Observations are point-in-time and apply only to this execution container and security context.

## What can and cannot be concluded

- [PDEATHSIG](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/PR_SET_PDEATHSIG.2const.html) applies to the process that sets it; it is cleared for a child after `fork`, and certain credential changes or privileged execs also clear it. Each process requiring this property must set and verify it. It is tied to termination of the creating parent **thread**, and may also fire on termination of subsequently assigned ancestor subreapers. If the parent dies before registration, no signal is retroactively guaranteed; set the signal and immediately compare expected/actual parent identity, then fail before work on mismatch. This check narrows an initialization race; it is not full containment or a proof of parent health for all future time.
- A [child subreaper](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/PR_SET_CHILD_SUBREAPER.2const.html) can adopt and wait on orphaned descendants while it remains alive; it does not automatically terminate them and is not inherited by ordinary forked children. Parent death and watchdog death can still leave a descendant outside observed cleanup. If wait status or descendant enumeration cannot be established, preserve **UNKNOWN**, not a successful clean-tree claim.
- A [pidfd](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/pidfd_send_signal.2.html) is a stable handle to the particular process, avoiding a later numeric-PID reuse mistake when sending through that handle. It is not a tree handle, does not prove ownership of all descendants, and does not map GPU/NVML host PIDs. The specific signal and termination/reap result must both be observed.
- A separate process group/session helps manage known cooperating members but is not a tree containment boundary. A suitably situated child may [create another session](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/setsid.2.html) or group and escape group-directed cleanup; check-then-`killpg` also retains a reuse/identity race. Never signal an unverified or shared group.
- Hierarchical resource controls require a genuinely delegated hierarchy and applicable ancestor limits; cgroup v2 ownership and controller rules are documented by the [Linux kernel](https://docs.kernel.org/admin-guide/cgroup-v2.html). No such boundary was demonstrated in this container.

## Minimal realistic design direction

Use a separate, reviewed owner/subreaper process with a bounded controller/worker protocol and durable start/finish/unknown evidence; obtain and retain a pidfd for each verified self-created direct process, use parent-death setup plus immediate parent-identity check in controlled worker startup, capture real wait status, and fail closed on failed/ambiguous monitoring. Protect against controller shutdown via a separately supervised owner/pipe and ensure descriptors are not accidentally inherited. These are **defense in depth for known processes**, not proof of whole-tree containment or guaranteed enforcement when the owner itself is killed or blocked. Preserve unobserved model/environment effects and unreaped or potentially escaped descendants as unknown and never assume zero costs.

If the actual model/runtime may fork uncontrolled descendants, requires a hard all-descendants termination guarantee, or cannot demonstrate reliable external supervision, this session's available evidence is insufficient: a properly delegated host/container supervisor is needed or the scientific design/claim must explicitly accept the documented uncertainty and stop on it. A fresh review and current resource check remain required before any real model call. Existing rev2 synthetic watchdog also needs review of the reported case where a child exits 0 before runtime control/timeout checks; actual child exit alone must not bypass required evidence and monitor-health checks. This report does not modify or approve that frozen candidate.
